You'll find cabins in Ohio near gorges and waterfalls in the Hocking Hills, river tubing at Mohican, small towns in Amish Country, long trails at Shawnee and a national park in Cuyahoga Valley. For most trips, we'd start in the Hocking Hills, about an hour southeast of Columbus.

Full disclosure: we host cabins in the Hocking Hills.

### The Hocking Hills

Pick the Hocking Hills if you want big hikes on the shortest drive from Columbus. The Ohio Department of Natural Resources (ODNR) points to the cliffs and hemlock-shaded gorges at [Hocking Hills State Park](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/hocking-hills-state-park), where the trails open a half hour before sunrise.

### Mohican

Mohican suits a group that wants to be on the water. ODNR says tubing is very popular on the Mohican River. The Clear Fork Gorge at [Mohican State Park](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/mohican-state-park) is more than 1,000 feet wide at the top and over 300 feet deep.

### Amish Country

Come here for slow drives and small-town stops more than long hikes. The Holmes County tourism bureau calls the county, whose seat is Millersburg, one of the largest Amish settlements in the world.

### Cuyahoga Valley

From Cleveland, Cuyahoga Valley is about 35 minutes away. [Cuyahoga Valley National Park](https://www.nps.gov/cuva/index.htm) follows a river valley between Cleveland and Akron, with the Towpath Trail on the route of the historic Ohio and Erie Canal.

### Shawnee State Forest

Shawnee is for hikers who want more than a day on the trail. [Shawnee State Forest](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/shawnee-state-forest) is the largest of Ohio's state forests, at more than 60,000 acres. ODNR calls it "The Little Smokies of Ohio." Its backpack trail runs 46.4 miles.

## Why we'd start in the Hocking Hills for cabins in Ohio

The main trails sit close together. Old Man's Cave, Cedar Falls and Ash Cave are a short drive apart, and our guide to [day trips from Columbus, Ohio](/blog/day-trips-from-columbus-ohio) fits all 3 into 1 day.

The drive is easy too. The Hocking Hills Tourism Association puts the region [roughly an hour southeast of Columbus](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/contact-us/maps-directions/), straight down US-33. A Friday check-in after work is realistic.

Off the trail, Lake Logan State Park has a swimming beach and boat rentals, and downtown Logan is where you'll go for dinner.

## State park cabins or a private rental

State park cabins put you inside the park. Hocking Hills State Park has a lodge and modern full-size cabins, and both book through the same state reservation site. Mohican State Park has deluxe vacation cabins and a lodge.

Our cabins are whole houses, with no other guests on the property. Most add a game room, a sauna or a court. You book ours direct with us.

## What to look for in cabin rentals in Ohio

- The beds. Check the bed list as well as the "sleeps" number. A cabin that sleeps 10 with 3 sofa beds is a different trip from one with 5 bedrooms
- The drive to the trails. 5 minutes and 40 minutes can both be "near Hocking Hills State Park." Ask which trailhead
- A private hot tub and the pool dates. Hot tubs usually run all year. Outdoor pools in Ohio are seasonal, so look for the exact dates
- Something to do at the cabin. A game room or a covered deck gives you somewhere to be on a rainy night, and a fire pit covers the clear ones
- Cell service and Wi-Fi. Signal can be spotty in the hills, so ask about Wi-Fi if you need to work
- The driveway in winter. Gravel and hills can need all-wheel drive in snow
- The house rules. Pets, visitors and quiet hours vary from cabin to cabin. Ours don't allow pets, parties or events
- The cancellation policy. Ours give a full refund up to 60 days before check-in, or 90 days at Loch Llyn and Valhalla Hills
- Who answers your messages. Ask who you'll talk to during the stay and how fast they reply
