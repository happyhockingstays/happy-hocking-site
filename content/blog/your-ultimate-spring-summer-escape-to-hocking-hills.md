Spring and summer in the Hocking Hills are pure magic. The trails come alive with wildflowers, the waterfalls run at their fullest and the forest canopy gives you shade for afternoon adventures. Planning a family reunion, a couples trip or a weekend with friends? Our cabins make a great home base for your warm-weather trip.

## Why spring and summer are made for the Hocking Hills

**Spring (March to May):** watch the forest wake up. The waterfalls are at their most dramatic after the winter melt, the temperatures are right for hiking (60°F to 75°F) and the crowds haven't arrived yet. Wildflowers carpet the forest floor, and you'll have the trails almost to yourself.

**Summer (June to August):** prime outdoor season. The weather is warm and inviting, made for swimming in your private pool, exploring caves, ziplining through the canopy or relaxing on the deck with a cold drink. Summer evenings around the fire pit with s'mores? The best.

## What makes our cabins great in warm weather

Skip the hotel. Our cabins give you space to spread out, private amenities and the freedom to set your own schedule. Here's what guests love most about spring and summer stays:

- **Your own pool.** Every one of our cabins has a private pool, open May 15 to October 15. At Clouds Reach it's heated and open April 15 to November 30
- **Outdoor spaces.** Decks, fire pits and outdoor seating for long summer evenings. Grill dinner while the sun sets over the hills, then gather around the fire as the stars come out
- **Comfortable interiors.** After a full day exploring, come home to air conditioning, a full kitchen and a comfy bed. No camping gear required

## Top spring and summer activities near our cabins

- **Hiking:** Old Man's Cave, Cedar Falls and Ash Cave are must-sees. Spring brings rushing waterfalls, and summer brings shaded trails that stay cool on hot days
- **Ziplining:** several zipline courses let you soar through the trees. Great for families with older kids and adventurous adults
- **Canoeing and kayaking:** the Hocking River is ideal for paddling. Rent gear locally or bring your own
- **Downtown Logan:** browse local shops, grab ice cream and enjoy small-town Ohio. The summer Saturday farmers market is a guest favorite
- **Caves:** the Hocking Hills are famous for recess caves and rock formations, and the cool air inside is welcome on a hot day

## Planning your stay

**For families:** look for multiple bedrooms, game rooms and room to roam. Our larger cabins sleep 16 to 29, ideal for multi-generational trips.

**For couples:** choose a cabin with a hot tub, a fireplace and a private view. Spring weekends are especially peaceful.

**For friend groups:** our mid-size cabins (sleeps 8 to 13) give you the right mix of shared space and private bedrooms. Think reunion trips, bachelorette weekends or birthday getaways.
