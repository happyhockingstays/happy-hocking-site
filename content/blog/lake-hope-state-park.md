Lake Hope State Park is a 2,983-acre park around a 120-acre lake, set entirely inside Zaleski State Forest in Vinton County, Ohio. Only paddles and electric motors are allowed on the water. Come for the beach, a rented kayak, the mountain bike trails and the ruins of Hope Furnace.

It's about 30 minutes by car from Ash Cave, at the south end of the Hocking Hills.

Hours and rentals change with the season, so check the [park page](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/lake-hope-state-park) from the Ohio Department of Natural Resources (ODNR) first.

## On the water at Lake Hope

### Boat rentals

The Lake Hope Boathouse rents canoes, kayaks, paddleboards, pedal boats and pontoons from spring to fall. Call 740-596-4949 for hours and prices.

Bringing your own boat? The lake has 1 boat ramp, along State Route 278 by the dam. With gas motors off the water, a kayak is the easy way to see it.

### The Lake Hope beach

The swimming beach is 600 feet long and sits near the dam. Swim only in the designated areas.

There's a beach house with restrooms, plus a sun deck and patio that overlook the lake and a concession stand. A boathouse shelter by the beach is first-come, first-served. Pets aren't allowed on the beach, and ODNR points swimmers to Ohio's BeachGuard site for water advisories.

### Fishing

Anglers catch bluegill, catfish, crappie and largemouth bass here. Anyone 16 or older needs an Ohio fishing license. The park page has a lake fishing map.

Download it before you go so you have it offline. For a long day, Zaleski State Forest around the park has a 29-mile backpacking trail. Register at the trailhead.

### Mountain biking

Lake Hope has 8 mountain bike trails covering more than 25 miles of steep hills and ravines, for intermediate and advanced riders. ODNR calls them one of Ohio's top single-track trail systems.

The longest are the Copperhead Trail at 7.2 miles and the Little Sandy Trail at 4.5 miles. The Hocking Hills Tourism Association has a [printable bike trail map](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/things-to-do/biking/lake-hope-bike-trails).

## Hope Furnace and the park's history

Hope Furnace was finished by 1854 as Big Sand Furnace, paid for by W.H. Allison. In the late 1860s it took the name of Hope, the company town to its east, where about 300 workers usually lived.

At its best, the furnace made 15 tons of iron a day, for cooking pots, railroad tracks and Civil War weapons. It closed in 1874.

Today 2 of its 3 stone chimneys still stand, and you can spot glassy slag on the ground nearby. The Works Progress Administration turned the land into the park between 1934 and 1939, according to ODNR. It opened as Lake Hope Forest Park and became one of Ohio's first state parks in 1949.

## Nature center, picnics and the seasons

The nature center sits near the campground, and its hours change with the season. In fall it's open 1:00 pm to 4:00 pm, Friday to Sunday.

Picnic areas with tables and grills are spread around the park. You can reserve the shelter house online or at 866-644-6727.

In spring, look for wildflowers like bloodroot, wild geranium and yellow lady's slipper. Beavers live here too, and in winter the park lists sledding, cross-country skiing, ice skating and ice fishing when conditions allow.

## A day at Lake Hope and Ash Cave

This is how we'd spend a day on the south side of the Hocking Hills:

- Start early at [Ash Cave](/blog/ash-cave), the short trail to a big recess cave
- Drive about 30 minutes east to Lake Hope. Stop at Hope Furnace on the way in
- Rent a kayak, or swim at the beach if it's summer
- Ride a loop of the Little Sandy Trail if you brought bikes
- End at Dunkle Creek with the hot tub and the fire pit

For more ideas across the area, see our guide to [things to do in Hocking Hills for adults](/blog/things-to-do-in-hocking-hills-for-adults). If you'd rather stay near Logan, [Lake Logan State Park](/blog/lake-logan-state-park) is just west of town.

## Is Lake Hope State Park worth the drive?

Pick Lake Hope State Park for no-gas-motor paddling, single-track trails and an iron furnace you can walk up to. If you'd rather have a lake near town with a marina, Lake Logan is the closer choice from Logan.
