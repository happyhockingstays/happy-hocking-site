## What to look for in large group cabin rentals in Ohio

Most people start by counting bedrooms. That matters, but with a big group it's only the first check. Here's what we look at:

- **Beds and bedrooms.** Count beds, not just bedrooms. Think about who shares. Bunk rooms are gold for kids and cousins
- **Bathrooms.** The morning rush is real. More baths means fewer people waiting in the hall
- **Kitchens and a big table.** A second kitchen or a table for 20 makes group meals easy instead of a project
- **Parking.** Big groups arrive in a lot of cars. Ask before you book
- **Gathering spaces and quiet corners.** You want room for everyone to be together, and somewhere to slip away
- **Things to do on site.** A pool, a game room or a court keeps a big group happy between outings
- **Drive time.** How far is it from home, and from the trails you want to hike?

## The questions we ask every group

When you reach out, we only need to know a few things to point you to the right place.

1. **How many people are coming?** This narrows things quickly. Our cabins sleep 6 to 29.
2. **What does your group love to do?** This is the one that matters most. Hiking and adventure, or rest and recovery? A pool and a sports barn, or a sauna and a quiet deck? Tell us what a great day looks like for your people.
3. **What brings you here?** A family reunion, a holiday together, a friends' weekend or a long-overdue reset. When we know the why, we can match you to the cabin that fits.
