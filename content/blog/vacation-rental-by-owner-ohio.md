## What a vacation rental by owner in Ohio really gets you

The thing we're proudest of isn't a feature on a listing. It's that when you book direct, you get us: open, complete communication with a real person who knows every one of these cabins by heart.

Before you book, we can help you make sure the cabin truly fits your group. Ask us anything and tell us what you're hoping for. We'll point you to the right home. We over-communicate on purpose, so we tend to take care of the little things before you think to ask.

You don't get that when a booking passes through a faceless booking site. The relationship is the whole point of a small family-owned business, and it's the part we'll never outsource.

## Book direct vacation rentals in Ohio: what you pay

Here's the straightforward version. When you book through a booking site, the site takes a fee. On Vrbo, a traveler service fee is added at checkout. Airbnb now takes its fee from the host instead of adding it at checkout. Either way, the booking site is part of every stay.

When you book directly with us, there's no booking site at all. No service fee at checkout, and no booking-site commission on your stay. It's the same cabin, the same care, the same cleaning team, stocked firewood and warm hot tub, just without the middleman.
