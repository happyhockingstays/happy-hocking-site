Family reunion ideas don't need to be complicated. The best reunions we host have a theme people can laugh about, a few big meals, 1 shared outing a day and plenty of time for cousins to just hang out. Here are the ideas that work, grouped so you can pick and choose.

We welcome big families to our cabins in the Hocking Hills every week. These are the things we see make a reunion weekend stick.

## Family reunion ideas that bring everyone together

Start with the ideas that cost nothing and need no setup. Most families need only 3 or 4 of these.

- **A welcome night.** Name tags, pizza and a fire pit on the first night, so the relatives who've never met can break the ice
- **1 big family dinner.** The one meal everyone sits down for, with the family's best recipes
- **A group hike.** One short trail, at the pace of the slowest walker
- **A family tree wall.** Tape a big sheet to the wall and let everyone add names, dates and photos
- **Story time with the elders.** Ask the oldest relatives 5 questions and record the answers on a phone
- **A recipe swap.** Every household brings 1 recipe card. Someone turns them into a family cookbook later
- **A group photo.** Same spot, every year, before anyone leaves

## Family reunion themes

A theme gives the weekend a name and the T-shirts a reason. Keep it to 1, and don't let it take over the planning. These family reunion theme ideas work in a cabin:

- **Family camp.** Call it Camp plus your last name, split into teams by household or by color, hand out bandanas and keep score all weekend
- **Back to our roots.** Built around the family tree, old photos and the stories behind them
- **Decades night.** Everyone dresses for the decade they were born in, and the playlist follows
- **The family Olympics.** A weekend-long competition with medals at the end. Our guide to [family reunion games](/blog/family-reunion-games) has the events
- **Into the woods.** A trail theme for an outdoor family, with a hike each day and a bonfire to close it out
- **The recipe reunion.** Every meal comes from a family recipe, and the cookbook is the souvenir

Family reunion themes work best when they're light. Name tags, a shirt and a sign are enough.

## Family reunion food ideas

Feeding 20 to 30 people for a weekend is the hardest part of a reunion. These family reunion food ideas keep anyone from spending the trip in the kitchen.

- **Give each household 1 meal.** They shop for it, cook it and clean it up
- **Build-your-own bars.** Tacos, baked potatoes, pasta or pizza. Set out the toppings and let people serve themselves
- **Make-ahead breakfasts.** Breakfast casseroles and overnight oats go in the night before
- **1 grill night.** Burgers and hot dogs for a crowd, with corn and watermelon
- **The family recipe dinner.** Grandma's casserole, an aunt's famous dessert. Save it for the big night
- **S'mores at the fire pit.** The dessert every age agrees on
- **A snack table.** Keep chips, fruit and drinks out between meals so nobody raids the dinner supplies

Do the big grocery run on the way in, then plan 1 quick top-up trip. A house with 2 kitchens helps a lot: [Loch Llyn](/cabins/loch-llyn) and [Valhalla Hills](/cabins/valhalla-hills) each have 2 full kitchens and sleep 29.

## Ideas for every age

A reunion spans 3 or 4 generations, so plan for the ends of the range.

**For kids:** a scavenger hunt around the property, a cousins-only craft table, a game room and pool time in season, May 15 to October 15 at most of our cabins. Let them run the scoreboard. [The Farm at Nickel Plate](/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate) has a sports barn and a basement game room, and [Lazy Deer Retreat](/cabins/lazy-deer-retreat) has a sport court and an outdoor playground.

**For teens:** give them a job with some status, like the weekend photographer or the person in charge of the dinner playlist. A ping pong tournament helps, too.

**For grandparents:** the best seat at the table, a short and level outing and a chance to tell the old stories. The paved quarter-mile trail to [Ash Cave](/blog/ash-cave) works for strollers and wheelchairs.

**For adults:** an evening around the fire pit after the kids are asleep, and a slow morning with coffee on the deck.

## Inexpensive family reunion ideas

A reunion doesn't need a big budget. The biggest savings come from sharing 1 house and cooking in.

- **Share 1 big house.** A large cabin puts everyone under 1 roof with a kitchen and a living room, which a block of hotel rooms can't do
- **Cook every meal in.** Assign meals by household and split the grocery bill
- **Use the free stuff.** Ohio's state parks are free to enter, and the trails in Hocking Hills State Park are open every day of the year
- **Make the souvenirs.** A family cookbook or printed group photos cost less than custom gear
- **Book direct.** At our cabins, booking direct skips the booking-site service fees

## Keep the memories

The photos and stories are what people take home.

- **One shared photo album.** Set it up before the trip and ask everyone to add their photos
- **A time capsule.** Everyone writes a note to open at the next reunion
- **The family tree, updated.** Photograph the wall before you take it down
- **The same group photo spot.** Next year, stand in the same place and compare

## Keep it a stay, not an event

One note from the hosts. Our cabins are for families staying together, not for events. Every guest counts toward the cabin's maximum, and parties aren't allowed. Visitors need host approval. If more relatives want to join for a single day than the cabin sleeps, a reservable shelter house at Hocking Hills State Park is a good spot for that day's picnic.
