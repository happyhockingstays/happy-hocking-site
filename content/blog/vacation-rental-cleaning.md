Vacation rental cleaning is a full reset of the home between one group's check-out and the next group's check-in. For a cabin, that means every room plus the hot tub, the pool in season, the fire pit and the decks, all done and checked in a few hours. Here's the cabin cleaning checklist we'd hand any owner.

We co-host vacation homes in the Hocking Hills, and every stay at them ends with a full clean. A thorough checklist is how a cabin earns 5-star cleanliness reviews week after week.

## Vacation rental cleaning starts with the clock

The turnover window is short. At most of our cabins, guests check out at 11:00 am and the next group checks in at 4:00 pm. That's 5 hours to clean, restock, check the hot tub and walk the home. At 2 of our cabins, check-out is 10:00 am.

That window shapes everything else:

- **Start with laundry.** Strip the beds and start the first load before anything else.
- **Bring enough people.** A home that sleeps 20 needs a bigger crew, not a longer day.
- **Keep spare linens on site.** A second full set means the beds can be made while the first set washes.
- **Know the home's quirks.** The breaker that trips, the window that sticks, the hot tub cover that needs 2 people.

## The cabin cleaning checklist, room by room

### Kitchen

- Run and empty the dishwasher, and put everything back where it belongs
- Wipe the counters, cabinet fronts, appliance handles and the table
- Clean the stovetop, oven, microwave, coffee maker and toaster
- Empty and wipe out the fridge and freezer, and toss anything guests left
- Take out the trash and recycling, and put in fresh bags
- Check the pantry basics guests count on: salt, pepper, cooking oil and coffee
- Sweep and mop the floor

### Bathrooms

- Scrub the toilet, shower, tub and sink
- Clean the mirrors and fixtures until they shine
- Put out fresh towels, bath mats and washcloths
- Refill toilet paper, soap, shampoo and conditioner
- Empty the trash, then sweep and mop

### Bedrooms

- Strip every bed, even the ones that look unused
- Make beds with fresh sheets and check the mattress protectors
- Check under the beds and in the drawers and closets for anything left behind
- Dust the nightstands, lamps and headboards
- Vacuum or sweep, including under the beds

### Living areas and game rooms

- Dust surfaces, shelves and the TV
- Vacuum the sofas and fluff the pillows and throws
- Wipe the remotes, light switches and door handles
- Put games, paddles and cues back where guests expect to find them
- Clean the fireplace glass and check the firewood

### Outside

- **Hot tub:** clear the debris, wipe the waterline and test the water. The [CDC recommends](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-swimming/prevention/preventing-legionella-from-hot-tubs.html) checking hot tubs with test strips for free chlorine of at least 3 parts per million (or bromine of at least 4) and a pH of 7.0 to 7.8. Close the cover when you're done
- **Pool:** skim, check the chemicals and straighten the loungers. Our cabin pools are open May 15 to October 15, except Clouds Reach, which is open April 15 to November 30
- **Fire pit:** clear the ash once it's cold, and stack fresh firewood
- **Decks and patios:** sweep, wipe the tables and chairs and pick up anything left in the yard
- **Grill:** scrape the grates and check the propane
- **Lights and heat:** set the thermostat for the season and leave the porch light on for guests arriving after dark

## What to restock every time

An Airbnb cleaning checklist that skips restocking is only half done. Keep a running list on site, and check it at every turnover:

- Toilet paper, paper towels and tissues
- Hand soap, dish soap, dishwasher pods and laundry detergent
- Shampoo, conditioner and body soap
- Trash and recycling bags
- Coffee, filters and creamer
- Firewood and propane
- Hot tub and pool chemicals
- A first aid kit and spare batteries for the remotes and smoke alarms

## Check for damage and lost items

Before the cleaners leave, someone should walk the whole home:

1. Look for anything broken, stained or missing, and photograph it with the date
2. Check that the doors, windows and locks work
3. Make sure the smoke and carbon monoxide alarms have power
4. Bag anything guests left, label it and tell the owner or co-host
5. Report problems the same day, so they're fixed before the next arrival

Photos taken at every turnover make it far easier to show when something happened and get it resolved quickly.

## Cabin cleaning is harder than a city rental

A cabin turnover has more ground to cover than an apartment. There's the hot tub, often a pool, a fire pit, decks, game rooms and sometimes a barn or a court. Our cabins sleep 6 to 29, and the biggest, [Loch Llyn](/cabins/loch-llyn) and [Valhalla Hills](/cabins/valhalla-hills), sleep 29 each. Many cabins also sit down long gravel drives, and cell service can be spotty in the hills, so the crew brings everything with them.

That's why so many owners hand cleaning off. With [vacation rental co-hosting](/blog/vacation-rental-co-hosting), the turnover, the hot tub and the restock all become someone else's job, and you see the results in your reviews.
