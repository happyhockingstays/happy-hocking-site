The main grocery stores in Logan, Ohio are a Walmart Supercenter on State Route 664 and a Kroger on Chieftain Drive, both open 6:00 am to 11:00 pm every day. There's also a Save A Lot in town. Near Old Man's Cave, Grandma Faye's covers snacks and anything you forgot.

Logan is the town on your way into the Hocking Hills from US-33, so plan the big shop there before you head out to the cabin.

## Walmart in Logan, Ohio

The [Walmart Supercenter in Logan](https://www.walmart.com/store/3282) sits on State Route 664 South, the road you take from US-33 toward the trails. It's open 6:00 am to 11:00 pm every day.

It's the one-stop option for a big order: groceries, a bakery and deli, a pharmacy and a Murphy gas station on the lot. Grocery pickup and delivery are available, so you can order from home and grab it on the way in. The pharmacy opens at 9:00 am.

## Kroger in Logan, Ohio

Logan's Kroger is at 31550 Chieftain Drive. It's open 6:00 am to 11:00 pm daily, with a pharmacy, bakery and meat counter.

Kroger offers pickup and delivery here too. The pharmacy line is (740) 380-2041, handy if someone in your group forgot a prescription refill.

## Save A Lot and other stops in town

Save A Lot, at 35107 Hocking Drive, is a smaller discount grocery store. It's good for budget basics. Check its hours before a late run.

Logan also has Dollar General and Dollar Tree stores. Use them for snacks and drinks between big shops.

From May to October, downtown Logan hosts a farmers market on select Saturday mornings. Check the dates before you plan a breakfast around it.

## Grocery stores near Hocking Hills trails

Grandma Faye's is a small grocery about 1 mile from Old Man's Cave, at 20507 State Route 664 South. It has groceries, a deli, a gift shop and camping supplies. The deli is handy for a trail lunch. That makes it the stop for lunch meat or the s'mores someone forgot.

Grandma Faye's is a top-up stop, so bring the main groceries with you.

## Shopping on the way in

You don't have to wait for Logan.

Coming from Columbus, Lancaster is on US-33 first. Its [Walmart Supercenter](https://www.walmart.com/store/3792) on North Memorial Drive keeps the same 6:00 am to 11:00 pm hours. It's the easy stop for guests at [Valhalla Hills](/cabins/valhalla-hills), which is in Lancaster.

Coming from Cincinnati, the route runs through Circleville, which has a [Kroger](https://www.kroger.com/stores/details/016/00844) on Lancaster Pike, a few minutes off the route. It's a good stop on the way to [The Johnny](/cabins/the-johnny) in Laurelville.

Once you're there, Laurelville has a Family Dollar on Laurel Street and Hillside Country Store on State Route 180 for basics. For the full routes, see our [directions to Hocking Hills, Ohio](/blog/directions-to-hocking-hills-ohio).

## What's already at the cabin

You don't need to buy the household basics. Every cabin comes with coffee, toilet paper, paper towels, shampoo, conditioner, body wash, dish soap and laundry detergent. Firewood for the fire pit is provided too.

Every cabin also has a grill, a coffee maker and cooking basics. Add these to the cart:

- Groceries for every meal you'll cook in, plus drinks and ice
- Breakfast for the first morning, so nobody has to drive out before coffee
- S'mores for the fire pit
- Plastic cups for the pool and hot tub, since glassware isn't allowed near either
- Anything special for the kitchen, like a favorite spice or hot sauce

Our [weekend trip packing list](/blog/weekend-trip-packing-list) covers the rest of the bag.

## A grocery plan for a big group

Feeding 16 to 29 people takes some planning. Start with 1 shared list split by meal, with 1 shopper per meal, so nobody brings the fourth jar of peanut butter. Then:

1. Order the staples for pickup at Walmart or Kroger in Logan, timed for the afternoon you arrive
2. Bring coolers in the car for the cold food, especially in summer
3. Plan 1 quick top-up run midway through the stay
4. Arriving on a holiday? Check the store's hours before you leave

Check-in is at 4:00 pm, so a pickup order at about 3:00 pm gets the cold food in the fridge on time.

The biggest cabins make the cooking easier too.

Valhalla Hills and [Loch Llyn](/cabins/loch-llyn) each have 2 full kitchens, so 2 cooks can work at once. Compare all our [cabin rentals for large groups](/hocking-hills-large-group-cabins). For splitting meals and costs, see [how to plan a group trip](/blog/how-to-plan-a-group-trip).

## Which cabins are closest to town

[Pickle Hill](/cabins/pickle-hill) and [The Farm at Nickel Plate](/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate) are about 5 and 10 minutes from downtown Logan. At The Johnny, top up in Laurelville. From [Dunkle Creek](/cabins/dunkle-creek), about 35 minutes from Logan, shop for the whole stay before you head out.
