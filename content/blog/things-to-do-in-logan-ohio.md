Things to do in Logan, Ohio start with the Hocking Hills trails 25 minutes south, but the town has more than people expect. There's a washboard factory you can tour, a museum of about 5,000 pencil sharpeners, a walkable Main Street and a 400-acre lake just west of town. Here's how we'd fill a day.

Logan is the county seat of Hocking County, founded by Thomas Worthington in 1816 and named for the Mingo chief James John Logan. It's also where most of our guests stock up on the way in.

## Things to do in Logan, Ohio at a glance

- **Tour the Columbus Washboard Company** downtown
- **See the Pencil Sharpener Museum** at the Hocking Hills Regional Welcome Center
- **Walk Main Street** for local shops, a gallery and lunch
- **Hunt for antiques** at the Logan Antique Mall and in nearby Rockbridge
- **Swim, paddle or fish** at Lake Logan State Park
- **Hike the gorges** of Hocking Hills State Park, a short drive south

## Attractions in Logan, Ohio

### The Columbus Washboard Company

The [Columbus Washboard Company](https://columbuswashboard.com/pages/visit-us-guided-washboard-factory-tours) has made washboards since 1895. It moved from Grandview, near Columbus, to Logan in 1999. Guided tours walk you through the factory floor, where some of the machines date to the 1800s.

Tours run at 11:00 am, 1:00 pm and 3:00 pm, last about 35 minutes and need a group of 4 or more. Call ahead to book (740-380-3828). Tickets are $8, $6 for seniors and active military and $5 for students and kids. The gift shop is at 4 East Main Street.

### The Pencil Sharpener Museum

Yes, really. When Rev. Paul A. Johnson retired in 1988, his wife gave him a few car-shaped pencil sharpeners to start a collection. By 2010 he had 3,450. Today the Paul A. Johnson Pencil Sharpener Museum holds an estimated 5,000, in a new building that reopened in January 2024.

It's at the [Hocking Hills Regional Welcome Center](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/contact-us/welcome-centers/), where US-33 meets State Route 664. The center is open 9:00 am to 5:00 pm Monday through Saturday and 11:00 am to 5:00 pm on Sunday. Pick up a trail map while you're there.

### Museums and rainy-day fun downtown

- **Hocking County Historical Society Museum:** an 1881 furnished Victorian home, a steam car, a railway telegraph office and more. Admission is free, with donations welcome. Hours are limited, especially in winter, so call ahead (740-385-6026)
- **The Bowen House:** a 19th-century home that's now an arts center, with rotating exhibits, music and workshops
- **Hocking Hills Children's Museum:** hands-on science and art stations and play zones for younger kids
- **Hocking Hills Escape Games:** puzzle rooms with local themes, good for a group on a wet afternoon

## Logan, Ohio shopping: Main Street and antiques

Logan, Ohio shopping is mostly small and local. Park once downtown and walk.

**On Main Street.** Gallery on Main, at 65 West Main Street, sells local art, crafts and food from Hocking Hills artists and doubles as a visitor stop. It's open 9:00 am to 5:00 pm Monday through Saturday. Nearby you'll find Simplicity Threads Boutique for clothing, Vintage en Vogue for vintage clothes and decor, Gifts That Give Back for fair-trade goods and Flowers by Darlene for flowers. Saving Hardware is an old-fashioned hardware store that also sells washboards.

**Antiques.** The Logan Antique Mall, on State Route 664 South, is the big one in town: vintage furniture, retro collectibles, jewelry and home decor. Just up US-33 in Rockbridge, Spring Street Antique Mall has about 120 dealers in a 12,000-square-foot building, and the Hocking Hills Market runs an outdoor flea market from April through October.

**The practical stores in Logan, Ohio.** Logan is where you'll find the supermarkets and gas stations for a cabin stay. Every trip to the trails passes through town, so plan your big grocery run here.

## Lake Logan and the parks in town

[Lake Logan State Park](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/lake-logan-state-park) is just west of town. The 400-acre lake allows motors up to 10 horsepower, with a 10 mph limit, so it stays calm for paddlers. The marina rents canoes, kayaks, paddleboards, pedal boats and pontoons in spring, summer and fall.

The swimming beach on the north shore runs 527 feet, is free and is open all year. There are no lifeguards, so keep an eye on kids. Anglers go after bluegill, crappie, bass, muskie and catfish, and anyone 16 or older needs an Ohio fishing license.

In town, the [city's parks](https://www.cityoflogan.oh.gov/recreation/page/parks-recreation-sites) are good for a picnic or a playground break. Mingo Park, on Homer Street, has ball diamonds, soccer fields and shelter houses. Kachelmacher Park is downtown at Mulberry and Falls streets. The city pool is at Market Street and Keynes Drive.

## Things to do near Logan, Ohio

The real headliners are a short drive south, in Hocking Hills State Park.

- **[Old Man's Cave](/blog/old-mans-cave):** the park's main trail and visitor center, about 25 minutes from downtown
- **Cedar Falls:** a 1-mile trail the park rates moderate, down to the falls
- **[Ash Cave](/blog/ash-cave):** a paved quarter-mile walk to the largest recess cave in Ohio
- **Conkle's Hollow:** a narrow gorge with cliffs nearly 200 feet high, and a partly paved trail to the falls
- **Rock climbing and rappelling:** the state forest sets aside 99 acres on Big Pine Road, about a mile east of Conkle's Hollow. Bring good gear and climb with experienced people

## A day in Logan, planned

- **Morning:** start at Old Man's Cave before the lot fills, then drive back toward town
- **Lunch:** downtown on Main Street. The local tourism association's list includes Home Tavern, The Feed, Pizza Crossing, M & M Family Diner, Hills Bagel Company, 58 West, Maya Burrito Co., Chef Moe's on Main and Shamrock Irish Pub
- **Afternoon:** the 1:00 pm or 3:00 pm washboard tour, then the antique mall
- **Late afternoon:** a swim or a paddle at Lake Logan, or the pencil sharpener museum if it's raining
- **Evening:** back to the cabin for the hot tub and the fire pit
