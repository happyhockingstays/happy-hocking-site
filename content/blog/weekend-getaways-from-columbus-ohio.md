Weekend getaways from Columbus, Ohio don't get much easier than this. The Hocking Hills feel a world away, but they're about an hour from downtown Columbus, so you can leave after lunch on Friday and be in the hot tub by dinner. Searching for a peaceful, scenic place to get away? The Hocking Hills are packed with nature, and they work for couples, families and friend groups alike.

## Why the Hocking Hills top weekend getaways from Columbus, Ohio

- **It's close.** About an hour from Columbus and under 3 hours from Cincinnati or Cleveland. No flights and no lost day on the road
- **It feels remote.** Trees, gorges and quiet roads. With spotty cell service in places and quiet all around, it's easy to unplug and reconnect with nature and each other
- **It's beautiful all year.** Spring brings wildflowers and rushing waterfalls. Summer brings shaded trails and cool river paddles. Fall is famous for its color, and winter turns the landscape into a quiet wonderland
- **It has cabins for every kind of trip.** Many come with hot tubs, fire pits and big views. Every one of [our cabins](/cabins) has a private pool, a hot tub and room to spread out

## Weekend trips from Columbus, Ohio: the drive

Most guests take US-33 southeast out of Columbus. It runs through Lancaster, then on to Logan, the gateway to the Hocking Hills trails. From Logan, the main trailheads are a short, winding drive away.

A few easy stops along the way:

- **Lancaster:** a historic downtown with shops and places to eat, about halfway there
- **Logan:** grocery stores, coffee and restaurants. Stock up here before you head to the cabin
- **Lake Logan:** just outside Logan, with boat, kayak and paddleboard rentals at [Lake Logan Marina](https://www.lakeloganmarina.com)

## A 2-night weekend getaway from Columbus

### Friday

Leave Columbus after lunch or right after work. Check-in is 4:00 pm. Pick up groceries in Logan, then settle in: light the fire pit, get in the hot tub and let the week fall away.

### Saturday

Start slow with coffee at [Hocking Hills Coffee Emporium](https://hockinghillscoffeeemporium.com/), then hit the trails while it's quiet. Old Man's Cave and Cedar Falls are connected by a trail, and Ash Cave is an easy walk for every age. In the afternoon, try a winery, a zipline tour or the pool back at the cabin. Bring dinner in from [Millstone BBQ](https://www.themillstonebbq.com/) and end the night at the fire pit.

### Sunday

A slow breakfast on the deck, one more short walk, then check-out at 11:00 am. You're home by early afternoon, which is the best part of a getaway this close.

## Road trips from Columbus, Ohio worth adding

Have an extra day? Make it a road trip:

- **Lake Hope:** about 30 minutes east of the main trails, with a quiet lake and forest all around
- **Athens:** a college town with its own energy, great restaurants and plenty to explore
- **Columbus Day weekend:** it lands in mid-October, right as the color builds. Our guide to [fall weekend getaways](/blog/fall-weekend-getaways) has the timing, and why to book early
