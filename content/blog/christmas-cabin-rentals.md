Christmas cabin rentals trade the hosting, the driving between houses and the crowded living room for one place where everybody sleeps under the same roof. In the Hocking Hills, that means a hot tub in the cold air, a fire pit under the stars and woods outside every window, about an hour from Columbus.

Here's how we'd help you choose a cabin, plan the days and book it without surprises.

## Why Christmas cabin rentals beat hosting at home

Nobody has to host. That's the first gift. There's no one house that gets taken over, no air mattresses in the hallway and no one driving home at 10:00 pm with sleeping kids.

The second is time. At a cabin, the plans are simple: a slow breakfast, a walk in the woods, a long soak, a big dinner. People who see each other twice a year end up talking for hours, because there's nowhere else to be.

And the third is the setting. If you've been picturing a Christmas cabin in the woods, with frost on the trees and a fire going, the Hocking Hills in December look a lot like that picture.

## What to look for in a Christmas winter cabin

For a Christmas winter cabin, the checklist is short.

- **Warm water outside.** A hot tub that stays open in winter. All of ours do.
- **A kitchen that can handle the big meal.** Count ovens and refrigerators, not just burners.
- **One room that holds everybody.** For presents, for dinner and for the movie afterward.
- **A bed for every person.** Check the room list on each cabin's page, down to the bunks.
- **A way in if it snows.** Most of our drives are easy. The drive at Rickenbacker Ridge is steep in one spot, so AWD or 4WD is a good idea there in snow.

Our guide to [cabins for winter](/blog/cabins-for-winter) goes deeper on saunas, snow and what to pack.

## What a Christmas cabin stay looks like

Here's a simple 3-night plan. Change anything you like.

**Day 1.** Check-in is at 4:00 pm. Unload the groceries, claim the bedrooms and put a pot of chili on. Hot tub after dark.

**Day 2.** Sleep in. Late breakfast, then a short hike while it's light. Ash Cave is level and paved, so everyone from toddlers to grandparents can make it. Board games and a fire in the afternoon.

**Day 3.** The big day. Presents, a long brunch, a walk around the property and dinner that takes all afternoon to make. End at the fire pit.

**Day 4.** Coffee, leftovers and check-out at 11:00 am, or 10:00 am at Valhalla Hills and Loch Llyn.

## Things to do in the Hocking Hills in December

You don't have to leave the cabin. But if you want to:

- **Christmas in Ash Cave.** The state park hosts this free evening in mid-December, usually on a Saturday from 5:00 to 7:00 pm. You walk a lit path to the cave, where there's a fire, hot chocolate and a visit from Santa. The park announces the date each year.
- **Frozen waterfalls.** When it stays cold, the waterfall at [Ash Cave](/blog/ash-cave) freezes into an ice formation at the base of the cave.
- **A quiet hike.** Winter is the quiet season on the trails. Wear boots with grip and be back before dark.
- **The Winter Hike.** If you're staying into the new year, the park's 6-mile Winter Hike runs each January, usually on the third Saturday.

## Booking Christmas cabin rentals: what to know

- **Minimum stay.** 2 nights at every cabin. Holiday dates may need 3.
- **How far ahead.** Calendars usually open about a year out. For cabin rentals for Christmas, earlier is better. The biggest cabins book earliest.
- **Paying.** You can pay in up to 3 installments, which helps when several households are splitting the cost.
- **Cancelling.** Most cabins offer a full refund up to 60 days before check-in and 50% up to 30 days before. Valhalla Hills and Loch Llyn use 90 and 60 days.
- **House rules.** No pets, no smoking and no parties or events. A family Christmas where everyone is staying at the cabin is exactly what these homes are for.
