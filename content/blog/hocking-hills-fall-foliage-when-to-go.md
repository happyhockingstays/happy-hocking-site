There's a stretch of the year when the Hocking Hills become almost unreal. The hillsides turn gold and crimson and deep orange. The air gets its first honest bite, and the whole region seems to glow. Step outside your cabin on a fall morning and you feel it right away, surrounded by color in every direction. Fall is the season people fall in love with here. The only trick is timing it, and booking in time.

We're a small family-owned business, and fall is the question we get asked more than any other. So here's our honest, local answer.

## When does the fall color peak in the Hocking Hills?

In most years, peak color lands in mid to late October, with the deepest color usually arriving in the last 2 weeks of the month. It builds gradually: you'll often catch the first turning leaves in late September, real color filling in through early and mid October, and the grand finale in that final stretch before the leaves drop in early November.

Here's the honest part, and it matters. This is Ohio, so the weather has a personality of its own and it can change fast. A warm spell can hold the green a little longer, and a cold snap or a windy week can move things along quicker than expected. Some years we get beautiful color and mild weather into early November. Think of mid to late October as your best bet rather than a guarantee, and keep an eye on the forecast as your trip gets close.

## The best trails to see the color

Almost every trail here turns spectacular in the fall, but a few are especially worth it. Choose based on your group's comfort level, and take your time. This is scenery you'll want to slow down for.

- **Ash Cave** is the gentle one: an easy, accessible walk to a massive recess cave framed by turning trees
- **Old Man's Cave** is the classic Hocking Hills hike, with gorges, waterfalls and color overhead
- **Conkle's Hollow** rewards a bit more effort on the rim trail, with long views over a valley full of color
- **Cantwell Cliffs** and **Cedar Falls** are beautiful this time of year too

For current trail conditions and maps, [Explore Hocking Hills](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/) is the resource we point every guest to.

## Make a full fall day of it

Fall here isn't only the hikes. After a morning on the trails, warm up with coffee at [Hocking Hills Coffee Emporium](https://hockinghillscoffeeemporium.com/) or the [Oasis Coffee Shop](https://www.hockinghillsoasiscoffeeshop.com/), and treat yourself to a cinnamon roll from [The Dough Maker](https://www.stellasmercantile.com/dough-maker), which you'll find at [Stella's Mercantile](https://www.stellasmercantile.com/).

An afternoon at a winery is pure fall: [Le Petit Chevalier](https://www.lepetitchev.com/) and [Hocking Hills Winery](https://www.hockinghillswinery.com/) are both beautiful, and [Rockmill Brewery and Distillery](https://rockmillbrewery.com/) sits on gorgeous grounds. For dinner, [Millstone BBQ](https://www.themillstonebbq.com/) and [Pizza Crossing](https://pizzacrossing.com/) are local favorites.

## Book earlier than you think

This is the most important tip we can give you. Fall is the busiest season in the Hocking Hills, and the best October weekends are claimed months in advance. We generally see fall and holiday weekends fill up 60 to 90 days out, and the peak-color dates go first. If you're dreaming about a fall trip, the time to book is now, not when the leaves are already turning.
