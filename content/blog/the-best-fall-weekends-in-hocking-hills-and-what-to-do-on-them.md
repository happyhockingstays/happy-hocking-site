A fall weekend in the Hocking Hills has a rhythm all its own. You drive in as the hills roll and the color deepens, step out of the cabin into crisp air and deep quiet, and for 2 or 3 days you get to be fully present in the most beautiful season this region has. We're a small family-owned business, and we love helping guests plan these weekends. Here's when to come and what to do while you're here.

## When are the best fall weekends?

In our experience, the best fall weekends run from mid-September through late October, with color usually peaking in the last couple of weeks of October. Early fall gives you comfortable hiking weather and the first turning leaves. Late October brings the full, glowing display. And because this is Ohio, gorgeous weather sometimes holds into early November, so don't count that window out either. Whenever you come, plan your days around the forecast and you'll be glad you did.

One honest heads-up: these are the most in-demand weekends of the year, and they book up early, often 60 to 90 days ahead. If a fall weekend is on your mind, claim your dates before someone else does.

## A perfect fall weekend, hour by hour

Here's the kind of weekend we'd plan for you.

### Friday evening

Arrive, settle in and let the drive melt off you. Light the fire pit, get in the hot tub and take in the fact that you're here. If you want dinner without the fuss, [Pizza Crossing](https://pizzacrossing.com/) is a beloved local spot. They've always been so kind to our guests.

### Saturday morning

Start slow with coffee at [Hocking Hills Coffee Emporium](https://hockinghillscoffeeemporium.com/) or the [Oasis Coffee Shop](https://www.hockinghillsoasiscoffeeshop.com/), and grab a cinnamon roll from [The Dough Maker](https://www.stellasmercantile.com/dough-maker) at [Stella's Mercantile](https://www.stellasmercantile.com/). Then head for the trails while the light is beautiful. Choose your hike by your group's comfort level: Ash Cave for an easy walk, Old Man's Cave for the classic gorge or the Conkle's Hollow rim trail for big views over the color. [Explore Hocking Hills](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/) has maps and conditions.

### Saturday afternoon

Warm up and slow down. An afternoon at a winery is the essence of fall here: [Le Petit Chevalier](https://www.lepetitchev.com/) and [Hocking Hills Winery](https://www.hockinghillswinery.com/) are both lovely, and [Rockmill Brewery and Distillery](https://rockmillbrewery.com/) has beautiful grounds. If your group wants a thrill instead, [Hocking Hills Canopy Tours](https://www.hockinghillscanopytours.com/) puts the fall canopy right below you.

### Saturday evening

Back to the cabin for a good meal. [Millstone BBQ](https://www.themillstonebbq.com/) is a favorite if you want to bring dinner in. Then the fire pit, the stars and each other.

### Sunday

Take one more easy walk or a scenic drive through the hills. If you have time, Lake Hope is about 30 minutes east and worth the little adventure. Then a slow morning on the deck before you go, promising yourselves you'll be back.
