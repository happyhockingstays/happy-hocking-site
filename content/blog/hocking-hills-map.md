The best Hocking Hills map for hiking is the state's free park map, a PDF revised in 2024 that shows every trail area, trail lengths, parking and restrooms. For roads and attractions, grab the [tourism association's](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/contact-us/maps-directions) free paper map at a welcome center. Save the park map to your phone and keep the paper map in the car, because cell reception can be patchy in the hills.

Hocking Hills State Park isn't one big park with one entrance. It's a string of separate trail areas, each with its own lot, so you'll drive between trailheads and the map is how you plan the order.

## Which Hocking Hills map you need

There are 3 maps worth having:

| Map | Made by | What it shows | Where to get it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hocking Hills State Park map | Ohio Department of Natural Resources | Every trail area, trail lengths, blaze colors, parking and restrooms | Free PDF on the state park page |
| Hocking State Forest map | ODNR Division of Forestry | The forest around the park, the Buckeye Trail, bridle trails and the rock climbing and rappelling area | Free PDF |
| Hocking Hills map | Hocking Hills Tourism Association | Roads, towns and attractions across Hocking County | Free at the welcome centers, or by request at 1-800-HOCKING |

For a hike, use the state park map. For finding your way between towns and attractions, the tourism association's map covers more ground.

## The Hocking Hills State Park map

The state's [Hocking Hills State Park map](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/ohiodnr.gov/documents/parks/parkmaps/hockinghillsparkmap.pdf) is the one to download. It opens with a regional overview of the park and Hocking State Forest, then zooms in on the main trail areas. Smaller inset maps cover Conkle's Hollow, Rock House and Cantwell Cliffs.

The trail table lists every trail by name, length and blaze color, with a difficulty rating of easy, moderate or difficult. It also marks which sections are one way and which are wheelchair accessible. The map shows parking lots, restrooms, picnic areas, shelter houses, the visitor center, the fire tower and the [John Glenn Astronomy Park](/blog/john-glenn-astronomy-park).

The safety notes printed on it are worth a read too. Stay on the trail and take extra care when it's muddy or icy. There's no swimming or wading in any of the water, and every waterfall the map names can be seen from the trail, so there's no reason to step off it.

The state's [Hocking Hills State Park page](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/hocking-hills-state-park) also links a trail map download, along with trail hours.

## How the trail areas connect

3 state routes tie the park together:

- State Route 664 runs south from Logan to Old Man's Cave
- State Route 374 links Cantwell Cliffs, Rock House, Conkle's Hollow and Cedar Falls
- State Route 56 passes Ash Cave at the south end

On the state's park map, the areas run north to south: Cantwell Cliffs, Rock House, Conkle's Hollow, Old Man's Cave, Cedar Falls and Ash Cave. Coming from Columbus, the north end is closest to US-33, where State Route 374 leaves the highway at Rockbridge.

By car, Rock House is about 15 minutes from Old Man's Cave and Cantwell Cliffs is about 25 minutes.

You can also walk between the 3 southern areas. The Grandma Gatewood Trail runs 6 miles from Old Man's Cave to Cedar Falls and on to Ash Cave, and it's part of Ohio's Buckeye Trail.

It doesn't loop back, so leave a car at the far end or ride back on the tourism association's free weekend shuttle. It has run late May to late October, stopping at Old Man's Cave, Cedar Falls and Ash Cave, so check this year's schedule first.

## Hocking State Forest and the other maps

The [Hocking State Forest map](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/ohiodnr.gov/documents/forestry/maps/hocking.pdf) covers the bigger forest that wraps around the park. It shows the forest and nature preserve boundaries, the bridle trails by color and the Buckeye Trail, marked with blue blazes. The rock climbing and rappelling area and the bridle trails each have their own detailed map from the forestry division.

The tourism association's free Hocking Hills map shows the bigger picture: towns, roads, shops, restaurants and attractions across the county. Pick one up at the [Hocking Hills welcome center](/blog/hocking-hills-welcome-center) on State Route 664 in Logan, or ask for them by phone at 1-800-HOCKING. Call the same number for directions to anything that isn't on their Hocking County map.

## Using a map when you lose signal

Cell reception can be patchy in the hills, so don't count on a live map in a gorge. A few habits help:

- Download the Hocking Hills State Park trail map PDF to your phone before you leave home
- Carry the paper map from the welcome center as a backup
- At each trailhead, check the direction signs before you start
- Plan to be off the trail before dark. The trails close a half hour after sunset
