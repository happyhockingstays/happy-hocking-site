Fall weekend getaways in the Hocking Hills have a rhythm all their own. You drive in as the hills roll and the color deepens, step out of the cabin into crisp air and deep quiet, and for 2 or 3 days you get to be fully present in the most beautiful season this region has. The hillsides turn gold and crimson and deep orange, and the whole region seems to glow. We're a small family-owned business, and fall is the question we get asked more than any other. Here's our honest, local answer on when to come and what to do while you're here.

## When are the best fall weekend getaways?

In our experience, the best fall weekends run from mid-September through late October. Color builds gradually: you'll often catch the first turning leaves in late September, real color filling in through early and mid October, and the grand finale in the last 2 weeks of the month, before the leaves drop in early November.

Here's the honest part, and it matters. This is Ohio, so the weather has a personality of its own and it can change fast. A warm spell can hold the green a little longer, and a cold snap or a windy week can move things along quicker than expected. Some years we get beautiful color and mild weather into early November. Think of mid to late October as your best bet rather than a guarantee, and plan your days around the forecast as your trip gets close.

Early fall gives you comfortable hiking weather and the first turning leaves. Late October brings the full, glowing display. Either way, you'll be glad you came.

## The best weekend getaways in October book early

This is the most important tip we can give you. Fall is the busiest season in the Hocking Hills, and the best October weekends are claimed months in advance. We generally see fall and holiday weekends fill up 60 to 90 days out, and the peak-color dates go first. If you're dreaming about a fall trip, the time to book is now, not when the leaves are already turning.

If you can do a midweek stay, that's the sweet spot: all the color with a fraction of the crowds.

## A fall weekend getaway, hour by hour

Here's the kind of weekend we'd plan for you.

### Friday evening

Arrive, settle in and let the drive melt off you. Light the fire pit, get in the hot tub and take in the fact that you're here. If you want dinner without the fuss, [Pizza Crossing](https://pizzacrossing.com/) is a beloved local spot. They've always been so kind to our guests.

### Saturday morning

Start slow with coffee at [Hocking Hills Coffee Emporium](https://hockinghillscoffeeemporium.com/) or the [Oasis Coffee Shop](https://www.hockinghillsoasiscoffeeshop.com/), and grab a cinnamon roll from [The Dough Maker](https://www.stellasmercantile.com/dough-maker) at [Stella's Mercantile](https://www.stellasmercantile.com/). Then head for the trails while the light is beautiful. Almost every trail here turns spectacular in the fall, so choose by your group's comfort level:

- **Ash Cave** is the gentle one: an easy, accessible walk to a massive recess cave framed by turning trees
- **Old Man's Cave** is the classic Hocking Hills hike, with gorges, waterfalls and color overhead
- **Conkle's Hollow** rewards a bit more effort on the rim trail, with long views over a valley full of color
- **Cantwell Cliffs** and **Cedar Falls** are beautiful this time of year too

[Explore Hocking Hills](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/) has maps and current trail conditions.

### Saturday afternoon

Warm up and slow down. An afternoon at a winery is the essence of fall here: [Le Petit Chevalier](https://www.lepetitchev.com/) and [Hocking Hills Winery](https://www.hockinghillswinery.com/) are both lovely, and [Rockmill Brewery and Distillery](https://rockmillbrewery.com/) has beautiful grounds. If your group wants a thrill instead, [Hocking Hills Canopy Tours](https://www.hockinghillscanopytours.com/) puts the fall canopy right below you.

### Saturday evening

Back to the cabin for a good meal. [Millstone BBQ](https://www.themillstonebbq.com/) is a favorite if you want to bring dinner in. Then the fire pit, the stars and each other, the last of the color fading into dusk.

### Sunday

Take one more easy walk or a scenic drive through the hills. If you have time, Lake Hope is about 30 minutes east and worth the little adventure. Then a slow morning on the deck before you go, promising yourselves you'll be back.

## The best cabins for a fall weekend

The right cabin turns a fall trip into something you'll talk about for years.

- [Clouds Reach](/cabins/clouds-reach) has a valley view and a heated pool open until November 30, so you can swim while the leaves turn
- [Dunkle Creek](/cabins/dunkle-creek) is a cozy A-frame in the woods with a hot tub and front-row fall color
- [Pickle Hill](/cabins/pickle-hill) sits on a hill with color all around, a sauna to warm up in after a crisp hike and an indoor court for an active group
- [The Johnny](/cabins/the-johnny) has kayaks on the pond, a covered porch and a gas fireplace for cool evenings, and the trees around the pond put on a show
- [Loch Llyn](/cabins/loch-llyn), [Valhalla Hills](/cabins/valhalla-hills) and [The Farm at Nickel Plate](/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate) give a big fall gathering room to spread out
