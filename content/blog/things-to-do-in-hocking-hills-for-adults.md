The best things to do in Hocking Hills for adults start before you arrive. From the moment the trees close in around you on the drive, your mood starts to shift.

There's a point on the drive in. Maybe you're talking to someone in the car. Maybe you're singing along to your music. The thoughts in your head are still going a mile a minute. Then the hills and the trees start to quiet the noise by their sheer presence. The roads get hillier, winding through the trees, a mini adventure that feels nothing like your everyday commute. By the time you pull in, you're surrounded by calm.

Why trust this guide? We were guests here once too. The nature, the trails, the roads and the community drew us in so much that we found a way to share them with you. Here's everything worth doing once you get here.

## The hikes worth getting up for

Before we get into specific trails, here's the best advice we can give you: check in with how you're feeling that day. Start with an easier hike, especially if your group needs that. Once you see how everyone feels, you can commit to the harder ones.

Weather is a big factor here. Summers get hot, so bring water. Winters can get icy, so wear the right shoes for slippery trails, dress in layers and bring gloves and a hat. One thing that surprises most visitors is the balance on the trails. You'll see other people out there, which feels good, but it never feels like you're on top of each other.

### [Ash Cave](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/parks-trails/parks/ash-cave/)

This is the one we recommend to everyone, whatever your fitness level. The trail from the parking lot is paved, short and easy enough for strollers and young kids. Don't let "easy" fool you, though. The cave is massive and beautiful in any weather. Depending on the day, you might see water, ice or fog, and each one makes it a completely different experience. Photographers love it for good reason.

### [Old Man's Cave](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/parks-trails/parks/old-man-s-cave/)

The famous one: the gorge, the Upper Falls, the Devil's Bathtub. There's a reason it's the most photographed spot in the Hocking Hills. Our advice is to go early on a weekday. On a Saturday afternoon in October, you'll share the trail with hundreds of people. At 8:00 am on a Tuesday, you might have it to yourself. The trail also connects to Cedar Falls if you want a longer hike, about 3 miles one way. [Lazy Deer Retreat](/cabins/lazy-deer-retreat) is just 5 minutes away, so you can beat the crowds.

### [Cedar Falls](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/parks-trails/parks/cedar-falls/)

A short, shaded walk to one of the prettiest waterfalls in the park. It's easy and always worth the stop, especially if you're already on the Old Man's Cave trails. After a good rain, the falls really come to life.

### [Conkle's Hollow](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/parks-trails/parks/conkles-hollow-nature-preserve/)

This one is special. The gorge trail at the bottom is flat and shaded, a walk between towering sandstone walls that feels like stepping into another world. If you're feeling strong, the rim trail above is more challenging and rewards you with some of the best overlooks in the region. If you only do 2 hikes on your trip, make this one of them.

### [Rock House](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/parks-trails/parks/rock-house/)

The only true cave in the park system: a cavern with window-like openings carved into the cliff face. The hike is moderate with some stone steps, and the cave is unlike anything else here. It tends to be less crowded than Old Man's Cave, especially during the week.

### [Cantwell Cliffs](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/parks-trails/parks/cantwell-cliffs/)

The least visited of the major trail areas, and the most adventurous. The trail drops through a narrow slot canyon that gets tight enough you'll need to turn sideways in spots. This one is for people who want to feel like they discovered something. The solitude alone makes it worth the drive.

## Unique things to do in Hocking Hills for adults

Once you've done the classic trails, these are the stops that make a trip feel like yours.

### Stargazing at John Glenn Astronomy Park

Don't miss one of the most underrated things to do in the Hocking Hills: stargazing at John Glenn Astronomy Park. With very little light pollution, it's an amazing place to see the night sky.

### Quieter waterfalls

Ash Cave and Cedar Falls are among the most visited, while Whispering Cave and Broken Rock Falls are quieter spots worth finding.

### Ziplining and paddling

[Hocking Hills Canopy Tours](https://www.hockinghillscanopytours.com/) sends you soaring on ziplines through the treetops. If your group loves the water, a [canoe or kayak trip on the Hocking River](https://hockingriver.com) is one of the best ways to spend a summer afternoon. Everyone comes back to the cabin sunburned, happy and ready for the hot tub.

### [Lake Logan](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/lake-logan-state-park)

This one catches people off guard. After a day on the trails, you don't expect to find a lake. Guests are always surprised when we tell them they can hike and boat in the same day, a few minutes from where they're staying. Lake Logan is a "dry" lake, which means no alcohol on the water. That keeps it calm, quiet and family-friendly. You can rent boats, kayaks and paddleboards at [Lake Logan Marina](https://www.lakeloganmarina.com).

If water on your own property matters to you, [Loch Llyn](/cabins/loch-llyn) has a private 2-acre lake and [The Johnny](/cabins/the-johnny) has a private 2-acre pond. Wake up, pour your coffee and walk to the water without getting in the car.

## Fun things to do near Hocking Hills, Ohio

### Wineries and breweries

[Hocking Hills Winery](https://www.hockinghillswinery.com/) is a local favorite with award-winning wines and live music on weekends. Great for a date night or a relaxed group evening out. Staying at [Dunkle Creek](/cabins/dunkle-creek)? [Le Petit Chevalier](https://www.lepetitchev.com/) is close by and worth the visit. Brewery 33 and [Rockmill Brewery and Distillery](https://rockmillbrewery.com/) round out the list. Pair your drink with a hearty meal at [Millstone BBQ](https://www.themillstonebbq.com/) or Kindred Spirits, 2 more local favorites.

### [Hocking Hills Coffee Emporium](https://hockinghillscoffeeemporium.com/)

Walking into Coffee Emporium feels like stepping into a storybook. It's unique and charming in a way that matches how relaxing the Hocking Hills already are. Hang out for hours or grab a cup and go. Either way, there's a sense of community that makes it more than a coffee shop. It's a short drive from most of our cabins and a great low-key start before a hike.

### [Oasis Coffee Shop, mini golf and the antique strip](https://www.hockinghillsoasiscoffeeshop.com/)

The Oasis Coffee Shop gives you that same warm feeling, and it's right next to a strip of shops that turns coffee into a full afternoon. There's mini golf and things for younger kids to do, so the little ones are entertained while everyone else browses the antique shops. We almost always find something to bring home. Visiting in the fall? It's a great place to start your holiday shopping.

### [Athens and Ohio University](https://www.ohio.edu/)

A lot of guests don't realize how close Athens is. It's a college town with its own energy, great restaurants and plenty to explore. We also host families visiting for Ohio University graduations, and our cabins give them a place to stay together that beats a hotel block.

## Hocking Hills indoor activities for a rainy day

Rain doesn't cancel the weekend. Some of the best indoor activities in the Hocking Hills are the ones above: a winery tasting room, a long coffee at the Emporium, the antique strip or lunch in Athens. Short hikes like Cedar Falls are at their best right after a rain, too.

Or don't leave at all. [Pickle Hill](/cabins/pickle-hill) has a regulation pickleball court in an enclosed barn, [The Farm at Nickel Plate](/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate) has a sports barn and a basement game room, and most of our cabins have a game room of some kind. Our [pickleball getaways](/blog/pickleball-getaways) guide has more on the cabins with courts.

## Start at the cabin

Honestly? The first thing we recommend is enjoying where you're staying.

Every one of our cabins has a pool and a hot tub. The pools are open May 15 to October 15 (Clouds Reach's heated pool runs April 15 to November 30), and the hot tubs are ready all year. Spend the morning by the pool. Soak in the hot tub in the afternoon. Play games, put your phone away, sit on the deck and listen to the woods. When the sun goes down, stargaze from the hot tub or the fire pit. You don't need a plan. You don't need to be anywhere.

The best Hocking Hills trips start with slowing down at the cabin before you ever get in the car.

## When to visit

Every season here feels like a completely different trip. That's one of the things that keeps guests coming back.

- **Spring:** the waterfalls are at their fullest and the forest floor comes alive with wildflowers. Trails can get muddy, so wear real hiking boots
- **Summer:** long days, warm nights and fireflies in the trees. The pools are open and the river is busy. See our [summer weekend getaways](/blog/summer-weekend-getaways) guide
- **Fall:** the color is some of the best in the Midwest, peaking in mid to late October. Weekends get busy, so a midweek stay is the sweet spot. Our [fall weekend getaways](/blog/fall-weekend-getaways) guide has the timing
- **Winter:** frozen waterfalls, snow on the trees and trails with almost no one on them. Some of our guests say it's their favorite season here. Our guide to [cabins for winter](/blog/cabins-for-winter) has more
