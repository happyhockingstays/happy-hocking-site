Family weekend getaways work best close to home, in 1 house that sleeps everyone, with a plan for rain. Good family trip ideas for 2 nights: a cabin with a pool, an easy state park trail, a lake day, stargazing, a game room or a court. Each one comes with a real example from our Hocking Hills cabins, which sleep 6 to 29.

## What makes good family weekend getaways

Family weekend getaways are short, so keep the drive short too. Pick a place you can reach after school on Friday. The Hocking Hills are about an hour from Columbus, 2.5 hours from Cincinnati and 3.5 from Cleveland.

After that, look for 3 things:

- Beds that fit your crew, including bunk rooms or a crib if you need one
- A plan for rain, like a game room or an indoor court
- Free things to do nearby, so the weekend doesn't add up fast

## Family vacation ideas for a 2-night weekend

Pick 1 or 2 of these vacation ideas for families and build the weekend around them.

### A cabin with a pool

Most of our cabin pools are open May 15 to October 15. Compare all our [cabins with private pools](/hocking-hills-cabins-with-pool).

At Clouds Reach, the saltwater pool is heated to about 88°F and open April 15 to November 30. Rickenbacker Ridge's pool is heated to about 82°F and open May 15 to October 15.

### An easy trail in a state park

The gorge trail at [Ash Cave](/blog/ash-cave) is a quarter mile, rated easy and wheelchair accessible, so strollers work too. Ohio's state parks charge no entrance fee, so the hike is free.

Stay on the marked trails and keep young children close, as the [state park](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/hocking-hills-state-park) asks.

### A lake day

[Lake Logan State Park](/blog/lake-logan-state-park) has a free 527-foot sand [beach](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/lake-logan-state-park) with no lifeguards. [Lake Logan Marina](https://www.lakeloganmarina.com/faq) rents kayaks, paddleboards and pedal boats.

Some cabins have their own water. Loch Llyn has a private 2-acre lake with kayaks and paddleboards, about 75 steps down from the house. The Johnny has a private 2-acre pond with kayaks.

### A night under the stars

[John Glenn Astronomy Park](/blog/john-glenn-astronomy-park), less than a mile west of Old Man's Cave, is free. From early March through Thanksgiving, volunteers run programs on Friday and Saturday nights around sunset, weather permitting. Program nights need a free parking pass.

### A rainy-day game weekend

[The Farm at Nickel Plate](/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate) has a basement game room with a pool table, air hockey, foosball, ping pong and video games.

Rickenbacker Ridge has foosball and air hockey. Dunkle Creek has shuffleboard and board games. See all our [cabins with game rooms](/hocking-hills-cabins-with-game-room).

### A sports weekend

[Pickle Hill](/cabins/pickle-hill) has a regulation pickleball court in its own barn, plus a basketball hoop and cornhole. The Farm's sports barn has an indoor court for basketball, pickleball and soccer. [Lazy Deer Retreat](/cabins/lazy-deer-retreat) has a sport court for pickleball and basketball.

### 2 families, 1 house

2 families of 5 or 6 fit in a cabin for 13 to 16: Clouds Reach and The Johnny each sleep 16, and Rickenbacker Ridge sleeps 13.

Bringing more? [Valhalla Hills](/cabins/valhalla-hills) and Loch Llyn each sleep 29. Compare our [cabin rentals for large groups](/hocking-hills-large-group-cabins).

## Family vacation ideas on a budget

Family weekend getaways cost less when the fun comes with the cabin:

- Loch Llyn's kayaks and paddleboards and The Johnny's kayaks come with the house, so there's no rental bill
- With a game room or court at the cabin, a rainy day doesn't need a paid outing
- Every cabin has a grill and cooking basics, so you can cook in. Our guide to [grocery stores in Logan, Ohio](/blog/grocery-stores-in-logan-ohio) covers the shopping
- The state park trails, the Lake Logan beach and the astronomy park cost nothing

## Picking a cabin for the kids

With kids along, check the size first. Dunkle Creek, Lazy Deer Retreat and Pickle Hill sleep 6 to 10.

Rickenbacker Ridge, Clouds Reach and The Johnny sleep 13 to 16. The Farm, Valhalla Hills and Loch Llyn sleep 22 to 29.

Then check the details:

- For babies and toddlers, The Farm has a crib, a high chair and stair gates
- A pack 'n play is listed at Dunkle Creek, Lazy Deer Retreat, Loch Llyn, Rickenbacker Ridge, Valhalla Hills and The Farm
- Loch Llyn, Valhalla Hills and The Johnny have high chairs too
- For bigger kids, Lazy Deer Retreat has an outdoor playground, plus children's books and toys
- The Farm has a swing set
- For sleepovers: a basement bunk room for 8 at Clouds Reach, a bunk room with a ladder-up loft at Rickenbacker Ridge
