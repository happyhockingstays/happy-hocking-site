Weekend getaways in Ohio don't need a flight or a 6-hour drive. The Hocking Hills give you sandstone gorges, waterfalls and cabins in the woods, about an hour from Columbus, 2.5 hours from Cincinnati and 3.5 from Cleveland. Here's why they top the list, and how to plan the trip for your season, your group and your budget.

We host weekend guests from every corner of the state. These are the trip ideas we hand them.

## Why the Hocking Hills top weekend getaways in Ohio

- **It's close.** About an hour from Columbus, so most of central Ohio can be here by dinner on a Friday
- **It's free to explore.** Ohio's state parks cost nothing to enter, and [Hocking Hills State Park](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/hocking-hills-state-park) is open every day of the year
- **It changes with the seasons.** Wildflowers in spring, shady gorges in summer, color in fall and ice in winter
- **The cabin is part of the trip.** Every one of our cabins has a private hot tub and a fire pit, and a pool that's open in season

The park's best-known trails are short. [Old Man's Cave](/blog/old-mans-cave) is a 1-mile gorge trail. [Ash Cave](/blog/ash-cave) is a paved quarter mile to the largest recess cave in Ohio. You can see both on a Saturday morning and still have the afternoon for the pool.

## Ohio getaways by season

Every season makes a different trip.

- **Winter:** quiet trails, frozen waterfalls and the hot tub after. Our guide to [cabins for winter](/blog/cabins-for-winter) has the plan, and our [Christmas cabin rentals](/blog/christmas-cabin-rentals) cover the holidays
- **Spring:** the waterfalls run their fullest and wildflowers come up on the gorge floors. Bring boots for the mud
- **Summer:** pool season, May 15 to October 15 at most of our cabins. Our [summer weekend getaways](/blog/summer-weekend-getaways) guide has the ideas
- **Fall:** the color peaks in October, and so does demand. See our [fall weekend getaways](/blog/fall-weekend-getaways) for timing and why to book early

## Adults-only getaways in Ohio

Our cabins aren't adults-only, but every stay is private. You get the whole house, the pool and the hot tub to yourselves, with no lobby, no shared deck and no other guests.

For a grown-up weekend, start early on a quiet trail, spend the afternoon at a winery or in the sauna and finish with the stars. The [John Glenn Astronomy Park](/blog/john-glenn-astronomy-park) is the only part of the state park open at night, with programs on clear Friday and Saturday nights from March to late November. ## Affordable Ohio getaways

A weekend away doesn't have to cost much. A few ways to keep it affordable:

- **Use the free stuff.** The state park trails, the Ash Cave walk and Lake Logan's swimming beach cost nothing
- **Split a bigger cabin.** A house for 16 shared by 4 couples or 3 families puts everyone under 1 roof with a full kitchen
- **Cook in.** Every one of our cabins has a full kitchen and a grill
- **Book direct.** Book with us and you skip the booking-site service fees

## Weekend vacation spots in Ohio near the Hocking Hills

Already been to the big trails? These weekend vacation spots in Ohio are an easy add from a Hocking Hills cabin.

- **[Lake Logan State Park](/blog/lake-logan-state-park):** a 400-acre lake just west of Logan, with boat rentals in spring, summer and fall and a free swimming beach
- **[Lake Hope State Park](/blog/lake-hope-state-park):** a 120-acre lake inside Zaleski State Forest, limited to paddling and electric motors. Its mountain bike trails run more than 25 miles
- **Tar Hollow State Park:** named for the pine tar early settlers took from its ridges, with a 15-acre lake, a 500-foot beach and a 21-mile backpacking trail
- **Conkle's Hollow State Nature Preserve:** a gorge only 100 feet wide in places, with cliffs nearly 200 feet high
- **Chillicothe:** the [Hopewell Culture National Historical Park](https://www.nps.gov/hocu/index.htm) protects earthworks built by the Hopewell people, and it's part of a World Heritage Site
