Here's the feeling everyone comes to the Hocking Hills for, whether they can put it into words or not. You step out of the cabin and the world goes quiet. You're surrounded by nature, the sounds of it all around you. It recharges you and leaves you feeling connected to something bigger. That feeling is why people come here, and it's why we fell in love with this place too.

The hard part isn't deciding whether to come. It's choosing where to stay when there are so many options and you only get this weekend once. We're a small family-owned business, and matching guests to the right cabin is our favorite part of what we do. So here's how we'd help you choose, the same way we would if you called us today.

## Start with the feeling, not the floor plan

Most people start by counting bedrooms. We start somewhere else: with what your group loves to do, because that's what makes a weekend great. A cabin isn't just a place to sleep. It's the setting for the memories you're about to make together.

So before anything else, picture it. Is your group the kind that wants to compete, keep score and stay moving from morning to night? Or the kind that wants to slow all the way down, sit by the fire and finally hear themselves think? Are you gathering the whole extended family under one roof, or slipping away as a couple for a few days? There's a cabin here for each of those, and the goal is to find the one with something for everyone in your group.

## The questions we ask every guest

When you reach out, we only need to know a few things to point you to the right place.

1. **How many people are coming?** Our cabins range from cozy A-frames for a couple or a small family to big homes that sleep 29, so this narrows things quickly.
2. **What does your group love to do?** This is the one that matters most. Hiking and adventure, or rest and recovery? A pool and a sports barn, or a sauna and a quiet deck? Tell us what a great day looks like for your people.
3. **What brings you here?** A milestone birthday, a family reunion, a company retreat, an anniversary or a long-overdue reset. When we know the why, we can match you to the cabin that fits the moment.

## Match your group to the right cabin

### For the big gathering: reunions, retreats and milestone groups

If you're bringing everyone, you want room for the whole group to be together and quiet corners for when people need a break.

[Loch Llyn](/cabins/loch-llyn) is our explore-together estate. It sleeps 29 and comes with things we love sharing with guests: a trail on the property down to a small waterfall, a private lake out back at the end of a rugged walk, paddleboards, a pool, a fire table on the big deck and a game room so long we call it the football room. It's our go-to for large family gatherings, company and wellness retreats and the occasional small wedding.

[Valhalla Hills](/cabins/valhalla-hills) also sleeps 29, high on a hill, but its gift is calm. This is our restore-together home, peaceful and private, with a sauna, a hot tub and the kind of quiet that's made for a wellness retreat or a multi-generational gathering that wants to exhale.

[The Farm at Nickel Plate](/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate) sleeps 22 and has our biggest pool plus a full sports barn. It's roomy and beautiful, and it works as well for a lively family reunion as for a company retreat that wants room for everyone.

![Private pool with lounge chairs along the stone wall at Valhalla Hills](/images/cabins/valhalla-hills/pool/valhalla-hills-pool-003.jpg)

### For active groups and families who can't sit still

If every gathering in your family turns into a tournament, we have your house.

[Pickle Hill](/cabins/pickle-hill) is our pickleball cabin, with a regulation court in its own barn, a basketball hoop, yard games, a pool and a sauna to recover in afterward. It sleeps 10, so it's ideal for a medium-size family or an active group that wants to play hard and then unwind. It sits in a quiet spot on a hill, so you get the energy inside and the peace outside.

[Clouds Reach](/cabins/clouds-reach) sleeps 16 and is our celebration cabin. Guests can't stop talking about the bocce court, and the heated pool has our longest season, open April 15 to November 30, so it's still an option long after summer ends. The valley view makes the whole place feel like an event.

[Lazy Deer Retreat](/cabins/lazy-deer-retreat) sleeps 8 and is the closest of our cabins to the trails, 5 minutes from Old Man's Cave, with a sport court, a game room and a sauna.

### For couples and quiet escapes

Sometimes it's just the 2 of you, and the whole point is to disconnect from everything and reconnect with each other.

[Rickenbacker Ridge](/cabins/rickenbacker-ridge) was made for this. It sleeps 13 but leads with romance: a heated pool, a hot tub with a view of the ridge and 20 wooded acres with no neighbors in sight. It's our favorite for couples and anniversaries, and it can still flex for a small group.

[Dunkle Creek](/cabins/dunkle-creek) is a cozy A-frame deep in the woods that sleeps 6, with a pool, a hot tub and forest at the end of the driveway. It's pure calm for couples and small families who want privacy.

### For personality and play

[The Johnny](/cabins/the-johnny) sleeps 16 and has character to spare, with a pool, kayaks on the pond, a covered porch and a gas fireplace. If your group wants a stay with a little swagger and a lot of fun, this is the one.

## Make it a real Hocking Hills weekend

Once you have your home base, the days fill themselves. Do the hikes first, and choose the trail that fits your group, because everyone has a different comfort level and the scenery is worth matching to the right path. Take your time. That's the part that stays with you.

Start your mornings slow with coffee. We love [Hocking Hills Coffee Emporium](https://hockinghillscoffeeemporium.com/) and the [Oasis Coffee Shop](https://www.hockinghillsoasiscoffeeshop.com/), and [Stella's Mercantile](https://www.stellasmercantile.com/) carries cinnamon rolls from [The Dough Maker](https://www.stellasmercantile.com/dough-maker) that you have to try. For a good meal, [Millstone BBQ](https://www.themillstonebbq.com/) and [Pizza Crossing](https://pizzacrossing.com/) are favorites, and Pizza Crossing has always been so kind to our guests. For a beautiful afternoon, try wine at [Le Petit Chevalier](https://www.lepetitchev.com/) or [Hocking Hills Winery](https://www.hockinghillswinery.com/), or a visit to [Rockmill Brewery and Distillery](https://rockmillbrewery.com/).

Families love the Children's Museum, the petting zoo, mini golf and [Hocking Hills Canopy Tours](https://www.hockinghillscanopytours.com/) for ziplining. Up for a little adventure? Lake Hope is about a 30-minute drive and worth it, and the scenic drive is a beautiful way to see the area. For trail details and conditions, [Explore Hocking Hills](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/) is the best resource.
