Searching for a peaceful, scenic place to get away? The Hocking Hills of Ohio are packed with nature, and they work for couples, families and friend groups alike.

## Natural beauty all year

The Hocking Hills are beautiful in every season. Spring brings wildflowers and rushing waterfalls. Summer brings shaded trails and cool river paddles. Fall is famous for its color, and winter turns the landscape into a quiet wonderland.

## Away from it all, and easy to reach

The Hocking Hills feel remote, but they're about an hour from Columbus and under 3 hours from Cincinnati or Cleveland. That makes them easy for a quick weekend or a longer stay.

## Cabins for every kind of trip

From rustic to roomy and modern, there are lots of ways to stay in the Hocking Hills. Many cabins come with hot tubs, fire pits and big views, which makes them great for a couples weekend, a trip with friends or a family reunion. Every one of [our cabins](/cabins) has a pool, a hot tub and room to spread out.

## Something for everyone

Into hiking, photography, stargazing or wine tasting? The Hocking Hills have it all. Families love the kid-friendly activities, and adults can enjoy quiet time away or a local winery tour.

## Disconnect to reconnect

With spotty cell service in places and quiet all around, the Hocking Hills make it easy to unplug and reconnect with nature and each other. It's one of the most relaxing places to get away in Ohio. [Find your cabin](/cabins) and see for yourself.
