Looking for cabins in Columbus, Ohio? Here's the short answer: the city itself has very few, and the ones worth the trip are about an hour southeast, in the Hocking Hills. That's where the woods, the hot tubs and the fire pits are, and it's close enough to leave after work and be in the water by dinner.

Most of our guests come from Columbus, so we've heard the question a lot. This is how we'd help a neighbor choose.

## Are there cabins in Columbus, Ohio?

Not many, and not the kind you're picturing. Columbus is a city of more than 900,000 people, and a cabin wants the opposite: trees, hills, dark skies and no one next door.

The good news is how little driving it takes to get there. Head southeast on US-33 and the land starts to roll around Lancaster. By Logan you're in the Hocking Hills, with sandstone gorges, waterfalls and cabins set back in the woods. So when people search for cabins in Columbus, this is usually what they end up booking.

## How far are the cabins from Columbus?

About an hour, depending on where in Columbus you start. Our cabins are spread across 5 towns, and the first ones you reach are the closest.

- **Lancaster.** [Valhalla Hills](/cabins/valhalla-hills) is about 45 minutes from Columbus, on a quiet hilltop between the city and the trails.
- **Sugar Grove.** [Rickenbacker Ridge](/cabins/rickenbacker-ridge) sits on 20 wooded acres between Lancaster and Logan, about 15 minutes from each.
- **Logan.** The heart of the Hocking Hills, about an hour from Columbus. Most of our cabins are here. See our guide to [Logan, Ohio cabins](/blog/logan-ohio-cabins).
- **Laurelville and McArthur.** [The Johnny](/cabins/the-johnny) and [Dunkle Creek](/cabins/dunkle-creek) are on the quieter west and south sides of the hills.

## The cabins close to Columbus, Ohio, for a quick trip

If the drive is what matters most, start with the 2 cabins close to Columbus, Ohio on the north side of the hills.

Valhalla Hills is the nearest, about 45 minutes out. It's a hilltop estate for 29, so it suits a family gathering or a few households traveling together. Rickenbacker Ridge sleeps 13, with no neighbors in sight and a game room for rainy afternoons. From either one, Lancaster's shops and restaurants are close, and Old Man's Cave is about 40 minutes farther south.

The trade-off is simple. Stay north and the drive from home is shorter. Stay near Logan and you're closer to the trails.

## What to do once you're here

You came for the cabin, so start there. But the trails are the reason these hills are famous, and 2 of them belong on a first trip.

- [Old Man's Cave](/blog/old-mans-cave) is a 1-mile gorge trail with 2 waterfalls and a huge recess cave. Go early.
- [Ash Cave](/blog/ash-cave) is a paved quarter-mile walk to the largest recess cave in Ohio. It works for every age.

Our guide to [weekend getaways from Columbus, Ohio](/blog/weekend-getaways-from-columbus-ohio) lays out a full 2-night plan, hour by hour. If you only have a day, [day trips from Columbus, Ohio](/blog/day-trips-from-columbus-ohio) are doable too, but you'll wish you'd stayed.

## When to come

There isn't a wrong season, only different ones.

- **Spring:** the waterfalls run full and the trails are quiet. Pools open May 15, and the heated pool at Clouds Reach opens April 15.
- **Summer:** pool season. The gorges stay shaded and cool on hot days.
- **Fall:** the color peaks in mid to late October in most years, and weekends book early.
- **Winter:** the quietest time of year. The hot tubs are open, 3 cabins have a sauna and the waterfalls freeze when it's cold enough.
