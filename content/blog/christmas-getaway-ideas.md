Looking for Christmas getaway ideas that don't need a flight, a week off or a spreadsheet? Start with a cabin a short drive from home (from the city, that means our [cabins in Columbus, Ohio](/blog/cabins-in-columbus-ohio) guide), add a hot tub and a fire pit, and build 2 or 3 days around doing very little. The Hocking Hills are about an hour from Columbus, and they're made for this kind of trip.

Here are 10 weekend getaway ideas for the holidays, with a cabin to match each one. Mix a few together and you have a trip.

## 10 Christmas getaway ideas for this year

### 1. Rent a cabin with a hot tub

It's the simplest plan on the list and the one people talk about afterward: cold air, hot water and nothing on the schedule. Every one of our cabins has a hot tub that's open all year. For a small group, [Dunkle Creek](/cabins/dunkle-creek) is a black A-frame in the woods with a covered hot tub and a fire pit.

### 2. Plan a sauna weekend

Hike in the cold, then sit in the heat. [Lazy Deer Retreat](/cabins/lazy-deer-retreat) has a private sauna, [Pickle Hill](/cabins/pickle-hill) has an infrared sauna and [Valhalla Hills](/cabins/valhalla-hills) has a 3-person sauna and an 8-person hot tub. Our guide to [cabins for winter](/blog/cabins-for-winter) has more on all 3.

### 3. Hike to a frozen waterfall

When it stays cold, the waterfall at [Ash Cave](/blog/ash-cave) freezes into an ice formation at the base of the largest recess cave in Ohio. The trail in is paved, level and a quarter mile long, so it works for every age.

### 4. Visit Santa in Ash Cave

Holidays in Ash Cave, long known as Christmas in Ash Cave, is a free afternoon the state park hosts in the cave. This season it's Saturday, December 12, 2026, from 3:00 to 5:00 pm, with holiday crafts, music, cookies, hot cocoa and Santa himself. The [park's event page](https://ohiodnr.gov/home/news-and-events/all-events/parks-wc-events/SE-Ohio/holiday-ashcave-hocking-12-12-26) has the details. Bundle up.

### 5. Put the whole family under one roof

Skip the hosting. A big cabin gives everyone a bed, one long table and no drive home after dinner. [Loch Llyn](/cabins/loch-llyn) and Valhalla Hills each sleep 29 and have 2 full kitchens. Our guide to [Christmas cabin rentals](/blog/christmas-cabin-rentals) walks through how to pick and book one.

### 6. Take the friends trip you keep postponing

The week between Christmas and New Year's Day is when everyone is finally off at the same time. [Clouds Reach](/cabins/clouds-reach) and [The Johnny](/cabins/the-johnny) each sleep 16, with room for a long dinner and a fire pit to end the night at.

### 7. Hold a family tournament

Some families relax. Others keep score. [Pickle Hill](/cabins/pickle-hill) has a regulation pickleball court inside its own barn, and [The Farm at Nickel Plate](/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate) has a sports barn for basketball and a game room with a pool table, air hockey and foosball. See all our [cabins with game rooms](/hocking-hills-cabins-with-game-room).

### 8. Hike Old Man's Cave without the crowds

In summer and fall, [Old Man's Cave](/blog/old-mans-cave) is one of the busiest trails in the park. In December it's far quieter. The trail is 1 mile, with stone steps, so wear boots with grip.

### 9. Cook one big meal together

Pick a dish that takes all afternoon and give everyone a job. A big cabin kitchen turns dinner into the activity. Bring the groceries with you, put on music and let the kids set the table.

### 10. Do nothing, on purpose

Bring books, a puzzle and good coffee. Sleep late. Watch the woods from the hot tub. Of all the winter getaway ideas here, this is the one people skip and the one they need most.

## How to turn these Christmas getaway ideas into a weekend

If you're collecting getaway ideas for a weekend, here's a plan that uses 4 of them.

**Friday.** Leave after work and check in any time after 4:00 pm. Dinner is something easy. Hot tub before bed.

**Saturday.** Slow morning. Hike Ash Cave or Old Man's Cave before lunch, then come back for the sauna or a game in the barn. Cook the big meal together. Fire pit after dinner.

**Sunday.** Coffee, a last soak and check-out at 11:00 am, or 10:00 am at Valhalla Hills and Loch Llyn.

Have an extra day? For 3 day getaway ideas, add a second trail and a lazy afternoon in Logan, or leave the day empty. Our guide to [weekend getaways from Columbus, Ohio](/blog/weekend-getaways-from-columbus-ohio) has a fuller 2-night plan.

## Last minute getaway ideas for December

Didn't plan ahead? These December getaway ideas still work.

- **Go midweek.** A Monday-to-Wednesday stay is easier to find than a weekend.
- **Go smaller.** A group of 6 or 8 fits most of our cabins, so you'll have more dates to choose from than a group of 29.
- **Stay close.** Anything within 3 hours of home means you don't lose a day to travel.
- **Check the calendar yourself.** Each cabin's page shows its open dates, and you can book on the spot.

Christmas getaway ideas work after December 25, too. The first weeks of January are just as cold and just as quiet, and the holiday rush is over. The state park's 6-mile [Winter Hike](https://ohiodnr.gov/home/news-and-events/all-events/parks-wc-events/SE-Ohio/winterhike-hocking-11627) is Saturday, January 16, 2027, from Old Man's Cave to Ash Cave.

## Good to know before you book a winter trip

- **Minimum stay:** 2 nights. Holiday dates may need 3.
- **Pools:** closed for the season. Most are open May 15 to October 15. Hot tubs are open all year.
- **House rules:** no pets, no smoking and no parties or events.
- **Snow:** most guests get in fine. If snow is in the forecast, message us and we'll help you plan your arrival.
