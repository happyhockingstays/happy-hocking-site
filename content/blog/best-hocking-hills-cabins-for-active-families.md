Some families don't do "sit around." You know the ones. The card game turns into a bracket. The backyard turns into a stadium. Somebody always keeps score, even when nobody agreed to keep score. And yet, the moment you step outside the cabin here, something shifts. You're surrounded by nature, and even the busiest group goes quiet for a second just to take it in. That's the magic of the Hocking Hills for an active family: room to move and reason to pause, all in the same weekend.

We're a small family-owned business, and matching a busy, playful group to the right home is one of our favorite things to do. If your people like to stay moving, here's where we'd send you.

## The cabins built for play

### Pickle Hill

[Pickle Hill](/cabins/pickle-hill) is our house for active groups. It has a regulation pickleball court in its own barn, plus a basketball hoop and yard games, a saltwater pool, a hot tub and an infrared sauna to recover in once the tournament ends. It sleeps 10 in a quiet spot on a hill, so you get all the energy inside and total peace outside. Play hard, then unwind.

### The Farm at Nickel Plate

Bringing the whole crew? [The Farm at Nickel Plate](/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate) sleeps 22 and has our biggest pool, along with a full sports barn for basketball, pickleball and soccer, plus cornhole and ping pong. It's roomy and comfortable, which makes it just as good for a lively family reunion as for a group that wants space to spread out.

![Indoor sport court in the sports barn at The Farm at Nickel Plate](/images/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate/sport-court/the-farm-at-nickel-plate-sport-court-003.jpg)

### Clouds Reach

[Clouds Reach](/cabins/clouds-reach) sleeps 16 and brings the celebration energy. The bocce court is the thing guests can't stop talking about, and the heated pool has our longest season, open April 15 to November 30, so it stays in play well past summer. The valley view makes every game feel like an event.

### The Johnny

[The Johnny](/cabins/the-johnny) sleeps 16 and has personality for days, with a pool, kayaks on its private pond, a covered porch and a gas fireplace. It's a favorite for groups who want a stay with a little swagger and a lot to do.

### Lazy Deer Retreat

[Lazy Deer Retreat](/cabins/lazy-deer-retreat) sleeps 8 and sits 5 minutes from Old Man's Cave, with a sport court, a game room, a pool and a hot tub. Hike first thing, then let the kids burn off whatever energy is left.

## Beyond the cabin: adventure for every energy level

The best part of an active family trip is that the Hocking Hills meet everyone where they are. Do the hikes first, and choose your trail by your group's comfort level. Ash Cave is an easy, stroller-friendly walk to a giant recess cave, while the Conkle's Hollow rim trail and Cantwell Cliffs give your more adventurous hikers something to earn. Take your time, because the scenery is the whole point. [Explore Hocking Hills](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/) has trail details and conditions.

When you want a thrill, [Hocking Hills Canopy Tours](https://www.hockinghillscanopytours.com/) sends you soaring on ziplines through the treetops. On warm days, get out on the water with a canoe or kayak trip, or spend an afternoon at Lake Hope, about 30 minutes east and worth the drive. Younger kids love the Children's Museum, the petting zoo and a round of mini golf.

You'll need fuel too. Start your mornings with coffee at [Hocking Hills Coffee Emporium](https://hockinghillscoffeeemporium.com/) or the [Oasis Coffee Shop](https://www.hockinghillsoasiscoffeeshop.com/). When everyone's hungry, [Millstone BBQ](https://www.themillstonebbq.com/) and [Pizza Crossing](https://pizzacrossing.com/) never miss. Pizza Crossing in particular has always been so kind to our guests.
