When you're planning a trip to the Hocking Hills, you'll find our cabins in a few different places online. So it's fair to ask: does it matter where you book? We think it does, and we want to be completely honest about why. We're a small family-owned business, and booking directly with us is the best way to experience our homes. Here's what it really gets you.

## First, and most important: a real relationship

The thing we're proudest of isn't a feature on a listing. It's that when you book direct, you get us: open, complete communication with a real person who knows every one of these cabins by heart.

Before you book, we can help you make sure the cabin truly fits your group. Ask us anything and tell us what you're hoping for. We'll point you to the right home. During your stay, if you need something, you reach an actual person who cares that your trip is everything you hoped. We over-communicate on purpose, so we tend to take care of the little things before you think to ask.

You don't get that when a booking passes through a faceless booking site. The relationship is the whole point of a small family-owned business, and it's the part we'll never outsource.

## Second, and honestly: it costs you less

Here's the straightforward truth. Third-party booking sites add service fees on top of the nightly rate, often somewhere around 10 to 15 percent. On a multi-night cabin stay, that can add $200 to $400 or more to your total. That money goes to the booking site, not to your trip and not to us.

When you book directly with us, those fees aren't there. It's the same cabin, the same care, the same cleaning team, stocked firewood and warm hot tub, just without the extra cost stacked on the end. We'd rather that savings stay with you.

![Hot tub on the deck at Lazy Deer Retreat](/images/cabins/lazy-deer-retreat/pool/lazy-deer-retreat-pool-008.jpg)

## What "handled" actually looks like

Booking direct also means the details are ours to carry, not yours. We stock firewood and propane, and we make sure the hot tub and pool are balanced and ready before you arrive. We keep an eye on the things guests should never have to think about, so the only thing left for you to do is arrive and exhale.

That care shows up in the small moments. We've had guests arrive a little unsure of what to expect and leave telling us it was one of the most restorative trips they've ever taken, that they finally felt connected to nature and to each other. Those stories are why we do this.

All of our cabins are on our [cabins page](/cabins), from cozy stays like [Dunkle Creek](/cabins/dunkle-creek) and [Rickenbacker Ridge](/cabins/rickenbacker-ridge) to big gathering homes like [Loch Llyn](/cabins/loch-llyn) and [Valhalla Hills](/cabins/valhalla-hills). Whichever one is calling your name, booking it direct is the way to get the best of it.

So talk to us. Tell us who's coming and what you love to do, and let us help you find the right fit. [Book direct](/cabins), or [reach out](/contact) with any question at all. We can't wait to host you.
