Cabins with pools in Ohio are open for swimming only part of the year, so start with the dates. Every one of our Hocking Hills cabins has its own private outdoor pool, open May 15 to October 15. Clouds Reach's heated saltwater pool runs April 15 to November 30.

## What to know about cabins with pools in Ohio

- The exact open dates, listed on each cabin page
- Whether the pool is heated
- Whether it's saltwater
- Shade and seating near the water
- A hot tub for the months when the pool is closed

You'll find 2 kinds of Ohio rentals with pools. At lodges and resorts, every guest shares the pool. At a rental house, only your group swims. Ours are rental houses, so each pool is private to your group.

## When our pools are open

Most of our pools are open May 15 to October 15, and Clouds Reach's runs April 15 to November 30.

- April 15 to May 14: Clouds Reach only
- May 15 to October 15: every pool
- October 16 to November 30: Clouds Reach only
- December 1 to April 14: pools closed, hot tubs open

Clouds Reach (April 15 to November 30) and Rickenbacker Ridge (May 15 to October 15) are heated, so their water is warmer early and late in the season. Only Clouds Reach opens earlier and closes later.

Every stay is 2 nights at least, and holiday and peak-season weekends may need 3. Tap a cabin name in the table to see its open dates.

## Picking a pool cabin for your group

Valhalla Hills, The Farm, Dunkle Creek and Lazy Deer Retreat all have pools open May 15 to October 15.

For a big group, look at the space around the water as much as the pool. Valhalla Hills has a huge patio deck for the people who'd rather watch. The Farm at Nickel Plate has a shaded pavilion next to its pool. Both sleep 22 or more.

For a small family, Dunkle Creek sleeps 6. Its pool looks out over a meadow and wooded hills. Lazy Deer Retreat sleeps 8.

For a group that wants games between swims, see our [cabins with game rooms](/hocking-hills-cabins-with-game-room).

## A pool day near the trails

Lazy Deer Retreat is about 5 minutes from Old Man's Cave and about 10 from Cedar Falls, so you can be on the trail before the crowds and back at the pool by afternoon. Dunkle Creek is about 15 minutes from Ash Cave, where the Gorge Trail is an easy, accessible quarter mile to a recess cave, according to the [Ohio Department of Natural Resources](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/hocking-hills-state-park).

From our other cabins, the main trails are 25 to 45 minutes away. Plan an early hike and an afternoon at the pool.

Every cabin has bath towels and pool towels, so leave those at home.

## A private lake, a pond and a public beach

If your crew wants to paddle as well as swim, 2 of our cabins have water of their own. Loch Llyn has a private 2-acre lake stocked with fish. Kayaks and paddleboards are provided, and the lake is about 75 steps down from the house.

The Johnny has a 2-acre pond off the back deck, with kayaks for guests.

For a public beach, [Lake Logan State Park](/blog/lake-logan-state-park) has a 527-foot swimming beach on its north shore, according to the [Ohio Department of Natural Resources](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/lake-logan-state-park). It's about 15 minutes from Clouds Reach and Pickle Hill.

## Pool safety with kids

None of our pools has a lifeguard, so an adult needs to watch the water. The Consumer Product Safety Commission's [pool safety steps](https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2025/CPSC-Provides-Important-Safety-Tips-This-Pool-Season-as-Americans-Dive-into-Summer) are a good checklist for a cabin weekend:

- Never leave a child alone in or near the water, and pick an adult Water Watcher who isn't on a phone
- Keep kids away from pool drains and other openings
- Know CPR, and teach kids to swim

Glassware isn't allowed near the pool or hot tub, so bring cans or plastic cups. For the hot tub side of the trip, see our [cabin rentals with hot tub](/blog/cabin-rentals-with-hot-tub) guide.
