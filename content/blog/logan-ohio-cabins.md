Logan, Ohio cabins give you the best of both sides of a Hocking Hills trip: the trails and waterfalls are a short drive one way, and groceries, coffee and dinner are a short drive the other. Our cabins here sleep 8 to 29, each with a private pool and a hot tub, and they're 5 to 20 minutes from downtown.

Logan is the town at the center of the Hocking Hills, about an hour southeast of Columbus on US-33. If it's your first visit, it's the easiest place to base yourself.

## Why stay in Logan

**It's central.** Old Man's Cave, Cedar Falls and Ash Cave are south of town, and Lake Logan is just west. From Logan, nothing is far.

**It has what you forgot.** Logan is where the grocery stores, gas stations and restaurants are. From Pickle Hill, that's a 5-minute run for ice or coffee, not a 40-minute round trip.

**It's the easy way in.** US-33 runs straight from Columbus to Logan, so the last stretch to your cabin is short.

## Lake Logan cabins: what to know

People often ask us about Lake Logan cabins. An honest answer first: our cabins aren't on the lake. The closest are about 15 minutes away.

The lake itself is worth an afternoon. [Lake Logan State Park](/blog/lake-logan-state-park) is about 3 miles west of town, with a 400-acre lake that's limited to small motors, so it stays calm. The marina rents canoes, kayaks, paddleboards, pedal boats and pontoons from spring through fall. The fishing is good, too: bluegill, crappie, bass, muskie and catfish. Anyone 16 or older needs an Ohio fishing license. The [Lake Logan State Park page](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/lake-logan-state-park) has the details.

If you'd rather have the water to yourself, [Loch Llyn](/cabins/loch-llyn) has a private 2-acre lake with kayaks and paddleboards. [The Johnny](/cabins/the-johnny), in nearby Laurelville, has a private pond with kayaks.
