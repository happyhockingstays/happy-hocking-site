Family reunion games are how 30 relatives who see each other once a year turn into one team again. The best ones need little setup, mix the ages and leave room for trash talk. Here are the games and activities that work at our cabins, outdoors and in, for toddlers to grandparents.

We welcome big families to the Hocking Hills every week, and the games are always what the kids remember. These are the ones that work.

## How to pick family reunion games

A few rules make any game work better for a reunion crowd:

- **Mix the ages on every team.** Kids with grandparents, teens with aunts. It's the point of the weekend
- **Keep the setup short.** If it takes more than 5 minutes to explain, save it for a smaller group
- **Make it a tournament.** A bracket on the wall turns a quick game into a weekend-long story
- **Have prizes, but small ones.** Dollar-store medals and a trophy that travels to next year's winner
- **Give someone the scoreboard.** A kid who doesn't want to play makes a great judge

## Outdoor family reunion games

When the weather's good, these family games for a family reunion get everyone outside.

- **Cornhole.** The reunion classic. Run a bracket with teams of 2, one older and one younger
- **Spikeball and KanJam.** Fast, loud and easy to learn. Both come with [Pickle Hill](/cabins/pickle-hill)
- **Bocce.** Slow enough for grandpa, competitive enough for the cousins. [Clouds Reach](/cabins/clouds-reach) has its own court, plus a big grassy field for yard games
- **Pickleball.** Pickle Hill has a regulation court in its own barn, and [Lazy Deer Retreat](/cabins/lazy-deer-retreat) has a sport court for pickleball and basketball. Our guide to [pickleball getaways](/blog/pickleball-getaways) has more
- **Basketball and soccer.** The sports barn at [The Farm at Nickel Plate](/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate) has an indoor court with hoops, a pickleball net and soccer goals
- **Water balloon toss.** Pairs stand facing each other and step back after every catch. The last pair with a whole balloon wins
- **Scavenger hunt.** Give teams a list: a pinecone, a feather, a photo with the oldest relative, a photo with the youngest. Kids can write the list

Pool games count, too, when the pool is open. Our cabin pools are open May 15 to October 15, except Clouds Reach, which opens April 15 and closes November 30. Every one of our [cabins with private pools](/hocking-hills-cabins-with-pool) also has a hot tub that's open all year.

## Indoor family reunion games

Rainy afternoons and late nights need indoor games. A cabin with a game room makes it easy.

- **Family trivia.** Write 20 questions about your own family: who was born where, whose first car was what and who met their spouse where. The older relatives win this one
- **2 truths and a lie.** Everyone tells 3 stories about themselves, and the room guesses which one is made up. You'll learn things about your relatives
- **Charades and Pictionary.** Teams of mixed ages, 1 minute a turn
- **Board game and card tournaments.** Most of our cabins stock board games. Spread 3 tables around the living room and rotate
- **Ping pong.** Pickle Hill, The Farm, [Loch Llyn](/cabins/loch-llyn) and [Valhalla Hills](/cabins/valhalla-hills) all have ping pong tables. Play to 11 and keep the bracket on the fridge
- **Pool, foosball and air hockey.** The Farm's basement game room has all 3, plus a dartboard. Loch Llyn and Valhalla Hills have pool tables, and [Rickenbacker Ridge](/cabins/rickenbacker-ridge) has foosball, air hockey and arcade games

Our page of [cabins with game rooms](/hocking-hills-cabins-with-game-room) lists the cabins with courts and game rooms side by side.

## Ideas for games at a family reunion, by age

Some ideas for games at a family reunion work best for one age group.

**For little kids:** a treasure hunt with clues, freeze dance, a sticker scavenger hunt and anything with bubbles. Keep it under 15 minutes.

**For teens:** a ping pong or pickleball bracket, a video challenge with the cousins and a photo hunt around the property. Teens will play if it's a competition and the adults aren't in charge.

**For grandparents:** cards, dominoes, family trivia and bocce. Give them a role, like judge or scorekeeper, if they'd rather watch.

**For everyone:** charades, a recipe or baby photo guessing game and the family Olympics.

## The family Olympics

The family Olympics turns a weekend of games into one big competition. Here's a simple plan.

1. **Make 4 to 6 teams.** Mix ages and households on every team, and let each pick a color and a name
2. **Pick 5 to 8 events.** Cornhole, a relay, trivia, ping pong, a scavenger hunt and a pool event if it's in season
3. **Spread them out.** 2 or 3 events a day, so there's still free time
4. **Keep a scoreboard.** Tape it to a wall where everyone walks by
5. **Close it out at dinner.** Hand out medals on the last night, and pass the trophy to next year's host

It's also one of the easiest family reunion themes. Our [family reunion ideas](/blog/family-reunion-ideas) have more themes, food ideas and ways to keep the memories.

## Family reunion activities beyond games

Not everything has to be a competition. A few family reunion activities everyone can share:

- **A group hike.** The paved, level quarter-mile trail to [Ash Cave](/blog/ash-cave) works for every age, strollers and wheelchairs included
- **Paddling.** Loch Llyn has a private 2-acre lake with kayaks and paddleboards. [The Johnny](/cabins/the-johnny) has a private pond with kayaks
- **A fire pit night.** Every one of our cabins has a fire pit. Bring s'mores and the family stories
- **Stargazing.** The skies get dark out here. Lie on the lawn after the kids are in bed
- **A family photo.** Same spot, every reunion
