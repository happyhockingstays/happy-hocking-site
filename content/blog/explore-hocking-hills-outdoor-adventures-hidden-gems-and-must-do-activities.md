The Hocking Hills of Ohio are a nature lover's dream in the Appalachian foothills. Known for dramatic cliffs, waterfalls and forested gorges, the region draws visitors from across the Midwest and beyond, all year round. Planning a weekend in Ohio or a longer trip? There's something here for everyone.

## Hocking Hills hiking trails

At the heart of the region is Hocking Hills State Park, home to some of the best hiking in Ohio. Popular trails include Old Man's Cave, Cedar Falls, Ash Cave and Rock House. Each has its own rock formations, waterfalls and views, and most are beginner-friendly, which makes them great for families.

## Waterfalls and photography

The waterfalls are a highlight for photographers and waterfall chasers. Ash Cave and Cedar Falls are among the most visited, while Whispering Cave and Broken Rock Falls are quieter spots worth finding.

## Outdoor adventures

Beyond hiking, paddle the Hocking River by canoe or kayak, or zipline through the treetops with [Hocking Hills Canopy Tours](https://www.hockinghillscanopytours.com/). Both are great for thrill-seekers and families looking for something to do together.

## Stargazing at John Glenn Astronomy Park

Don't miss one of the most underrated things to do in the Hocking Hills: stargazing at John Glenn Astronomy Park. With very little light pollution, it's an amazing place to see the night sky.

## Local wineries and breweries

After a day of exploring, unwind at [Hocking Hills Winery](https://www.hockinghillswinery.com/) or Brewery 33. Pair your drink with a hearty meal at [Millstone BBQ](https://www.themillstonebbq.com/) or Kindred Spirits, 2 local favorites.
