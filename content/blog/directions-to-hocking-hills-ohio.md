Directions to Hocking Hills, Ohio start with US-33 for most drivers. From Columbus, take US-33 East about an hour to the State Route (SR) 664 exit, signed Logan and Bremen, and head south to Old Man's Cave.

From Cincinnati, come in from the west on SR 56 through Laurelville. From Cleveland, pick up US-33 in Lancaster.

## Where is Hocking Hills?

The Hocking Hills are a region of sandstone gorges, waterfalls and forest in southeastern Ohio. On the state's park map, the trail areas sit inside [Hocking County](https://hocking.oh.gov/index.php/node/13). Logan, the county seat, is the town to aim for.

[Hocking Hills State Park](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/hocking-hills-state-park) is a set of separate trail areas south and west of Logan. The state lists 7 of them.

[Old Man's Cave](/blog/old-mans-cave) and the park's visitor center are on SR 664. Most of the other areas sit along SR 374, and [Ash Cave](/blog/ash-cave) is off SR 56.

## Hocking Hills location and drive times

The [Hocking Hills Tourism Association](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/contact-us/maps-directions) puts the region roughly an hour southeast of Columbus. Plan on these times by car:

| Starting from | About how long | Main roads | Where you reach the hills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Columbus | 1 hour | US-33 | Logan (SR 664 exit) |
| Cincinnati | 2.5 hours | I-71, US-35, US-22, SR 56 | Laurelville |
| Cleveland | 3.5 hours | I-77, I-70, US-33 | Logan (SR 664 exit) |

## Directions to Hocking Hills, Ohio from Columbus

From Columbus, it's about an hour on US-33 East to the SR 664 exit. The tourism association gives this route:

1. Take US-33 East out of Columbus toward Lancaster
2. At Lancaster, stay on the US-33 bypass for the fastest drive. Or take the Lancaster Business Route (Memorial Drive) if you want to stop for food or shopping in town
3. Continue on US-33 to the SR 664 exit, signed Logan and Bremen
4. Turn right onto SR 664 South. The Hocking Hills Regional Welcome Center is just past the intersection
5. Stay on SR 664 South to reach Old Man's Cave and the park's visitor center

Heading to [Cantwell Cliffs](/blog/cantwell-cliffs) or [Rock House](/blog/hocking-hills-rock-house) first? Leave US-33 earlier at SR 374 in Rockbridge, then follow SR 374 southwest into the park.

Staying near Laurelville, on the west side of the hills? Skip US-33. Take US-23 South to Circleville, then SR 56 East to Laurelville.

Heading home, take SR 664 North back to US-33 West. Our guide to [weekend getaways from Columbus, Ohio](/blog/weekend-getaways-from-columbus-ohio) plans the full weekend.

## From Cincinnati and Dayton

From Cincinnati, the tourism association routes you in from the west:

1. Take I-71 North to the US-35 East exit at Washington Court House
2. Follow US-35 East, then US-22 East to Circleville
3. In Circleville, take SR 56 East to Laurelville
4. At the circle in the center of Laurelville, take the second road on the right to stay on SR 56 East toward South Bloomingville and Ash Cave

That route puts Ash Cave first. For Logan and the regional welcome center instead, stay on US-22 East past Circleville toward Lancaster, pick up US-33 East and take the SR 664 exit. Our [day trips from Cincinnati](/blog/day-trips-from-cincinnati) guide routes the drive through Chillicothe, with stops on the way.

From Dayton or Indianapolis, take I-70 East to Columbus, then US-33 East through Lancaster to SR 664.

## From Cleveland and Pittsburgh

The tourism association's route from Cleveland, Akron and Canton skips Columbus:

1. Take I-77 South to Cambridge
2. Take I-70 West to the Lancaster exit (exit 126)
3. Follow State Route 37 into Lancaster, turn right onto US-22 for 4 blocks, then left onto US-33 East
4. Take US-33 to the SR 664 exit and turn right

Your navigation app may send you down I-71 through Columbus instead. Either way, you finish on US-33 and SR 664.

From Pittsburgh, take I-79 South to I-70 West, through Zanesville to the State Route 13 exit. Head south on SR 13, jog west on State Route 256, then take SR 664 South. You'll cross US-33 at the Logan-Bremen interchange, pass the regional welcome center and stay on SR 664 South to the trails.

## Which road goes to which trail

Once you're in the hills, 3 state routes connect the trail areas:

- SR 664 runs south from Logan to Old Man's Cave and the park's visitor center
- SR 374 links Cantwell Cliffs, Rock House, Conkle's Hollow and [Cedar Falls](/blog/cedar-falls-hocking-hills)
- SR 56 runs past Ash Cave at the south end of the park

Cedar Falls is just past Old Man's Cave on SR 374. For every trailhead and the official park maps in one place, see our [Hocking Hills map](/blog/hocking-hills-map) guide.

## The last few miles

A few tips for the last few miles on smaller state routes:

- Save your directions before you leave home. Cell reception can be patchy in the hills
- Type in the trail area's name, like "Ash Cave" or "Cedar Falls," rather than a guess at a street address. Then follow the state park signs
- On summer and fall weekends, a [free shuttle](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/hocking-hills-shuttle) run by the tourism association and Logan Transit links downtown Logan with the trail areas

Our guide to the [Hocking Hills welcome center](/blog/hocking-hills-welcome-center) covers parking, the shuttle and all 3 visitor centers, including Laurelville's.
