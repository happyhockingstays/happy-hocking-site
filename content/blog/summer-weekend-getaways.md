Summer weekend getaways in the Hocking Hills are pure magic. The forest canopy shades the trails, the caves stay cool on the hottest days and the evenings stretch out long and warm around the fire pit. Best of all, every one of our cabins has its own pool. Planning a family reunion, a weekend with friends or a trip with the kids? Our cabins make a great home base for your warm-weather trip.

## Why summer weekend getaways belong in the Hocking Hills

Summer is prime outdoor season here. The weather is warm and inviting, made for swimming in your private pool, exploring caves, ziplining through the canopy or relaxing on the deck with a cold drink. Fireflies come out in the trees after dark. Summer evenings around the fire pit with s'mores? The best.

It's close, too. The Hocking Hills are about an hour from Columbus and under 3 hours from Cincinnati or Cleveland, so a Friday-to-Sunday trip doesn't lose a day to the drive. Our guide to [weekend getaways from Columbus, Ohio](/blog/weekend-getaways-from-columbus-ohio) has a 2-night plan.

Coming in late spring? The pools open May 15, the waterfalls are still running full from the spring rains and the crowds haven't arrived yet.

## Things to do in Ohio this summer, Hocking Hills style

- **Hiking:** Old Man's Cave, Cedar Falls and Ash Cave are must-sees, and the shaded gorges stay cool on hot days. Go early to beat the heat and the crowds
- **Caves:** the Hocking Hills are famous for recess caves and rock formations, and the cool air inside is welcome in July
- **Ziplining:** [Hocking Hills Canopy Tours](https://www.hockinghillscanopytours.com/) sends you soaring through the trees. Great for families with older kids and adventurous adults
- **Canoeing and kayaking:** the [Hocking River](https://hockingriver.com) is ideal for paddling. Rent gear locally or bring your own
- **Lake Logan:** rent a boat, kayak or paddleboard at [Lake Logan Marina](https://www.lakeloganmarina.com). It's a "dry" lake, with no alcohol on the water, so it stays calm and family-friendly
- **Downtown Logan:** browse local shops, grab ice cream and enjoy small-town Ohio. The summer Saturday farmers market is a guest favorite

For a longer list, our guide to [things to do in Hocking Hills for adults](/blog/things-to-do-in-hocking-hills-for-adults) goes trail by trail.

## Your own pool: what makes a summer vacation cabin

Skip the hotel. A cabin gives you space to spread out, private amenities and the freedom to set your own schedule. Here's what guests love most about summer stays:

- **Your own pool.** Every one of our cabins has a private pool, open May 15 to October 15. At [Clouds Reach](/cabins/clouds-reach) it's heated and open April 15 to November 30, and at [Rickenbacker Ridge](/cabins/rickenbacker-ridge) it's heated to about 80°F
- **Outdoor spaces.** Decks, fire pits and outdoor seating for long summer evenings. Grill dinner while the sun sets over the hills, then gather around the fire as the stars come out
- **Room to play.** [Pickle Hill](/cabins/pickle-hill) has a regulation pickleball court in its own barn, and [The Farm at Nickel Plate](/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate) has a sports barn for basketball, pickleball and soccer. See our [pickleball getaways](/blog/pickleball-getaways) guide for more
- **Comfortable interiors.** After a full day exploring, come home to air conditioning, a full kitchen and a comfy bed. No camping gear required

## Places to visit in Ohio in summer, a short drive away

Once you're settled, a few easy half-day trips round out the weekend. Lake Hope is about 30 minutes east of the main trails, with a shaded lake and forest all around. Downtown Logan is 5 to 25 minutes from most of our cabins, and Lake Logan is close by. On a clear night, the dark skies around the Hocking Hills make for great stargazing, right from the hot tub.
