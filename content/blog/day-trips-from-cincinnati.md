Day trips from Cincinnati usually mean an hour or 2 on the road. Head east instead and you trade the river for sandstone gorges, ancient earthworks and the largest recess cave in Ohio. The Hocking Hills are about 2.5 hours away, so it's a long day, but the stops along the way make the drive part of the trip.

We host plenty of guests from Cincinnati. Here's the day we'd plan for them, and when we'd tell them to stay the night instead.

## The best day trips from Cincinnati, heading east

Chillicothe sits most of the way from Cincinnati to the Hocking Hills, roughly 107 of the 140 miles. That makes it the natural place to split the drive. Pick 1 or 2 of these stops and you have a full day.

### Hopewell Culture National Historical Park, Chillicothe

The [Hopewell Culture National Historical Park](https://www.nps.gov/hocu/index.htm) protects earthworks and mounds built by the Hopewell people, a culture that flourished between 2,200 and 1,500 years ago. Its sites are part of the Hopewell Ceremonial Earthworks, named the nation's 25th World Heritage Site in 2023.

Start at the Mound City Group, home to the park's only visitor center, at 16062 State Route 104 in Chillicothe. The [visitor center](https://www.nps.gov/hocu/learn/historyculture/mound-city-group.htm) is open 9:00 am to 4:00 pm every day except Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day, and the grounds are open dawn to dusk.

### Great Seal State Park

The hills at [Great Seal State Park](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/great-seal-state-park) are the ones on the Great Seal of Ohio, which gives the park its name. It has more than 23 miles of trails above the Scioto Valley. For a short, hard climb, the Sugarloaf Mountain Trail rises almost 500 feet in less than a quarter mile, on a 0.8-mile route.

### Tar Hollow State Park

On the last stretch into the hills, near Laurelville, Tar Hollow is named for the pine tar early settlers took from the pines on its ridges. It has a 15-acre lake for paddling, a 500-foot swimming beach with no lifeguard and the Homestead Trail, a 2.5-mile loop the park calls family friendly.

### The Hocking Hills trails

Coming from the west, [Ash Cave](/blog/ash-cave) at the south end of the state park makes a good first stop. A paved, level quarter-mile trail leads to a sandstone horseshoe about 700 feet across. From there, Cedar Falls and [Old Man's Cave](/blog/old-mans-cave) are a short drive north, or a walk along the Grandma Gatewood Trail if you have the legs and a ride back.

## A Hocking Hills day from Cincinnati, planned

This plan puts the long drive at the ends of the day and the trails in the middle.

- **7:00 am:** leave Cincinnati and drive east
- **About 9:30 am:** Ash Cave, while the lot is quiet
- **Late morning:** Cedar Falls or Old Man's Cave, then lunch at a trailhead picnic table
- **Afternoon:** a second trail, or Logan's Main Street for coffee and a look around
- **Before dark:** the trails close a half hour after sunset, so head out then
- **Evening:** the drive home, about 2.5 hours

Want to break it up? Stop at Mound City on the way out and save the hills for the afternoon. Our guide to day trips from Columbus, Ohio has more trail plans that work from any direction.

## Cincinnati day trips for couples and families

**For couples:** a slow morning at the earthworks, the short trail to Ash Cave and a sunset before the drive. On clear Friday and Saturday nights from March to late November, the John Glenn Astronomy Park, the only part of the state park open at night, holds stargazing programs. You need to register for parking.

**For families:** keep the trails short and the stops frequent. Ash Cave's paved trail works for strollers, and Tar Hollow's beach and the general store's mini golf, open seasonally, give kids a break from the car.

**For hikers:** Great Seal's climbs and the 6-mile Grandma Gatewood Trail from Old Man's Cave to Ash Cave, which the park rates difficult.

## Before you go

- **Check the hours.** Hocking Hills State Park's trails open a half hour before sunrise and close a half hour after sunset. The Mound City visitor center closes at 4:00 pm
- **Wear real shoes.** Most Hocking Hills trails have stone steps and wet rock. In winter, the park suggests ice cleats
- **Pack lunch.** Every trailhead parking area in the state park has picnic tables and grills
- **Download your maps.** Cell service comes and goes in the hills, so save directions before you lose signal

## When a day trip from Cincinnati should be a weekend

Here's the honest part. 5 hours in the car for 6 hours on the trails is a lot. If you're coming from Cincinnati, a weekend lets you hike the best trails early, before the lots fill. You still have time for the pool and the fire pit.

Our cabins on the west and south side of the hills are the easiest from Cincinnati. [The Johnny](/cabins/the-johnny), in Laurelville, sleeps 16 and has a private pond with kayaks, about 25 minutes from Ash Cave. [Dunkle Creek](/cabins/dunkle-creek), a black A-frame for 6 near McArthur, is about 15 minutes from Ash Cave. [Lazy Deer Retreat](/cabins/lazy-deer-retreat) is about 5 minutes from Old Man's Cave.

Every stay is 2 nights at least, with check-in at 4:00 pm. For more trip ideas around the state, see our guide to [weekend getaways in Ohio](/blog/weekend-getaways-in-ohio).
