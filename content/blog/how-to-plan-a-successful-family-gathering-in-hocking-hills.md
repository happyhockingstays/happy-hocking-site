Planning a family reunion or a multi-generational trip? The Hocking Hills are one of the best places in Ohio to bring everyone together, with big cabins, outdoor fun for every age and plenty of quiet.

## Find the right cabin

Start with a home that fits your group. Look for multiple bedrooms, a big kitchen, bunk rooms, game rooms and outdoor extras like a hot tub and a fire pit. The right cabin keeps everyone close while still giving people private space.

For the biggest groups, [Loch Llyn](/cabins/loch-llyn) and [Valhalla Hills](/cabins/valhalla-hills) each sleep 29, and [The Farm at Nickel Plate](/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate) sleeps 22. Not sure which fits? [Ask us](/contact). Matching groups to cabins is our favorite part of the job.

## Plan activities for every age

Make a list of things to do that work for everyone. Short hikes like Ash Cave are stroller-friendly, while older kids might love ziplining or canoeing. Plan outings around mealtimes to keep everyone energized.

## Share meals and traditions

Make a meal plan and split up the cooking so everyone pitches in. Eat together around the fire pit or the big dining table. Think about starting a tradition, like a family talent show or a game night.

![Foosball and air hockey tables in the basement game room at The Farm](/images/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate/basement-game-room/the-farm-at-nickel-plate-basement-game-room-004.jpg)

## Respect personal space

One of the best things about a big cabin is room to breathe. Give people time to relax on their own or explore the area at their own pace. Building in downtime keeps the mood light.

## Capture the memories

Bring a tripod and take a group photo at a scenic overlook. Set up a shared photo album, or make a photo book after the trip. Those are the moments everyone will look back on.
