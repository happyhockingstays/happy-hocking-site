The Hocking Hills welcome center is the Hocking Hills Regional Welcome Center, just off US-33 at State Route 664 in Logan. It's open 9:00 am to 5:00 pm Monday to Saturday and 11:00 am to 5:00 pm on Sunday. The state park's own visitor center is farther south, at Old Man's Cave. Entry to the park is free.

## The Hocking Hills welcome center and visitor centers at a glance

The Hocking Hills Tourism Association runs [3 welcome centers](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/contact-us/welcome-centers/) in Hocking County. The Ohio Department of Natural Resources runs the state park's visitor center. Its third center is a small one at 65 West Main Street in downtown Logan.

| Center | Who runs it | Where | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hocking Hills Regional Welcome Center | Tourism association | 13178 State Route 664 South, Logan, just off US-33 | 9:00 am to 5:00 pm Monday to Saturday, 11:00 am to 5:00 pm Sunday |
| Hocking Hills State Park visitor center | Ohio Department of Natural Resources | Old Man's Cave parking lot, State Route 664 | 10:00 am to 5:00 pm daily |
| Hocking Hills Visitor Center at Laurelville | Tourism association | 16197 Pike Street (State Route 56), Laurelville | Thursday to Monday in summer, Friday to Sunday in winter |

Start at the regional welcome center if you're coming down US-33 and want maps and local tips. Start at Old Man's Cave if you're going straight to the trails.

## The Hocking Hills Regional Welcome Center in Logan

The regional welcome center is just south of the US-33 and State Route 664 interchange. Coming from Columbus on US-33, take the State Route 664 exit, signed Logan and Bremen, and turn right (south).

Inside, staff hand out brochures and the tourism association's free Hocking Hills maps. They'll also give you directions. Our [Hocking Hills map](/blog/hocking-hills-map) guide explains which map covers what.

The center opened in May 1994. About 45,000 visitors a year stop in.

The center's grounds also hold the Paul A. Johnson Pencil Sharpener Museum. It reopened in a new building in January 2024. Admission is free, the Columbus Dispatch reported.

More on the museum and the rest of Logan is in our guide to [things to do in Logan, Ohio](/blog/things-to-do-in-logan-ohio).

## The Hocking Hills State Park visitor center at Old Man's Cave

The Hocking Hills State Park visitor center sits at the Old Man's Cave parking lot off State Route 664. The state's [park page](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/hocking-hills-state-park) lists it as open daily from 10:00 am to 5:00 pm.

The building opened in 2019, with about 8,500 square feet inside and 5,000 square feet of covered verandas. The upper level helps you plan your day across the park, the nearby state forest and the nature preserves. The gift shop is upstairs too.

The lower level has exhibits on the region's history and ecology, plus restrooms and water bottle filling stations.

For questions before your trip, call the park office at (740) 385-6842, 8:00 am to 4:30 pm on weekdays. The [Old Man's Cave](/blog/old-mans-cave) trail starts a short walk from the building.

### The 2026 visitor center fire

A small fire broke out in an office at the visitor center on the evening of April 22, 2026, after closing. Smoke and water damage kept it closed for cleanup through at least May 3, [the Scioto Post](https://www.sciotopost.com/?p=446626), a local news site, reported.

The trails stayed open.

## The visitor center in Laurelville

Coming from Cincinnati or the west? The tourism association's smaller center is in a log cabin on State Route 56 in Laurelville. It shares a parking lot with the Laurelville Fruit Farm.

Its official name is the Hocking Hills Visitor Center at Laurelville, but it isn't the state park's visitor center at Old Man's Cave. The center opened in 2003 and helps about 4,500 visitors a year. Its hours change with the season:

- Summer, May 15 to November 14: Thursday 2:00 pm to 5:00 pm, Friday and Saturday 10:00 am to 5:00 pm, Sunday 11:00 am to 5:00 pm and Monday 10:00 am to 3:00 pm
- Winter, November 15 to May 14: Friday and Saturday 10:00 am to 4:00 pm, Sunday 11:00 am to 4:00 pm and the Monday after a holiday, 10:00 am to 3:00 pm

## Hocking Hills State Park tickets, hours and trail rules

You don't need a ticket. Ohio's state parks charge no entrance fee. The Ohio Department of Natural Resources says admission, parking and programs are free at every Ohio state park. That covers the trails, the lots and the visitor center.

The trails and picnic areas open a half hour before sunrise and close a half hour after sunset, every day of the year. In late fall and winter, plan on a short hiking day. For the rest of the trip, see our guide to [things to do in Hocking Hills for adults](/blog/things-to-do-in-hocking-hills-for-adults).

The park stays open in winter, but the state warns the trails are often icy and recommends ice cleats. A few more rules from the park:

- Stay on the marked trails at all times
- Many trails are one-way, so follow the direction signs
- Keep young children close

## Hocking Hills State Park parking

Each trail area has its own free lot: Old Man's Cave (next to the visitor center), Cedar Falls, Ash Cave, Rock House and Cantwell Cliffs.

Lots near the trails fill on busy weekends. The tourism association and Logan Transit run a [free weekend shuttle](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/hocking-hills-shuttle) from late May to late October.

It starts at the courthouse in downtown Logan and stops at Old Man's Cave, where a second loop runs to Cedar Falls and Ash Cave. The shuttle is wheelchair accessible.

## Plan your first stop

If you only have time for 1 stop, make it the state park visitor center at the Old Man's Cave trailhead. Catch the Hocking Hills welcome center on the drive in. Both centers are free, and so is the park.
