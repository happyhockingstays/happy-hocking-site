Cantwell Cliffs is the farthest north of the Hocking Hills State Park areas, and usually the quietest. It's a sandstone cliff about 150 feet high, curved like a horseshoe, with a recess cave under the rim and a trail that winds through narrow gaps between fallen blocks of rock. If you've done Old Man's Cave and want something with fewer people and more scramble, this is the one.

## Cantwell Cliffs at a glance

- **Where:** State Route 374 near Rockbridge, about 20 minutes from downtown Logan
- **Trail:** about 2 miles, rated difficult by the park, with steep stone steps
- **Hours:** a half hour before sunrise to a half hour after sunset, every day
- **Cost:** free, parking included
- **At the lot:** picnic tables, grills, restrooms and a shelter house you can reserve
- **Best for:** steady hikers who don't mind steps and tight spots

## Cantwell Cliffs, Hocking Hills: what you'll see

The state's geologists describe it as "a gigantic precipice, 150 feet high and in the shape of a horseshoe." A stream called Buck Run carved it, cutting back into the hill over a very long time and undercutting the softer middle layer of the sandstone.

That softer layer is where the recess cave sits, under an overhang about 30 feet thick. It formed the same way as the caves at [Old Man's Cave](/blog/old-mans-cave) and [Ash Cave](/blog/ash-cave), just on a quieter, rougher stretch of the park.

Below the cliff, big blocks of rock have split off and slumped to the valley floor. Look closely at them and you'll see honeycomb weathering, small holes where loose sand washed out of the stone.

## The Cantwell Cliffs trail

The park lists the Cantwell Cliffs trail at 2 miles and rates it difficult.

The trail follows the rim, drops on stone steps to the valley floor and runs along the base of the cliff. In places it threads between the fallen blocks and the main cliff wall. The narrowest gap has a nickname that's lasted at least since the 1950s, when state geologists wrote it down: the Fat Woman's Squeeze. Most adults turn sideways to fit.

A few tips:

- **Wear shoes with real grip.** The steps are steep and the rock stays damp in the shade
- **Leave the big pack in the car.** A small daypack is easier in the tight spots
- **Stay on the marked trail.** The park requires it, and the cliff edges here are high
- **Skip it in ice.** In winter, the park recommends ice cleats on every trail. On this one, an icy day is a good day to go somewhere flatter

## Parking and getting there

It's on State Route 374, the road that links most of the park's trail areas. Navigation apps list it as Cantwell Cliffs, Ohio 374, Rockbridge, OH 43149. The lot is at the trailhead.

It's the farthest of the major areas from the rest of the park: about 25 minutes by car from Old Man's Cave and about 20 from downtown Logan. That distance is part of why it's quieter.

Like every trail area in the park, the lot has a picnic area with tables, grills and restrooms. A day-use shelter house can be reserved through [Reserve Ohio](https://reserveohio.com).

## A piece of the park's history

In 1933, the Civilian Conservation Corps set up 2 work camps in the park. Camp Logan, Company 526, was a camp of Black workers. It sat on top of these cliffs. The [park's history](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/hocking-hills-state-park) credits them with improvements at Cantwell Cliffs, Rock House and Rock Bridge, and the CCC camps built many of the trails and bridges still in use today. Camp Logan closed in 1937.

## When to go

- **Spring:** wildflowers on the valley floor and water running off the rocks
- **Summer:** the shaded valley stays cooler than the parking lot
- **Fall:** color on the rim, and usually fewer people than the trails farther south
- **Winter:** beautiful with ice, but the steps and passages get slick. Go only with ice cleats or save it for spring

## Who it's for and who should skip it

This trail is for hikers who like a little adventure: steps, tight spots and a cliff overhead. It's a good second or third trail on a trip, once you've seen the bigger names.

It isn't the trail for strollers, wheelchairs, toddlers on foot or anyone with bad knees on a long staircase. For that crew, the paved walk to Ash Cave or the partly paved Gorge Trail at Conkle's Hollow, about 1 mile round trip, are better picks.

## Pair it with a nearby stop

The cliffs make a good half day with another stop nearby.

- **Rock House** is the park's only true cave, a 200-foot passage inside a cliff, also on State Route 374
- **[Rockbridge State Nature Preserve](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/rockbridge-state-nature-preserve)** has the largest natural bridge in Ohio, a sandstone arch spanning 50 feet, on 2.8 miles of trail
- **Downtown Logan** has lunch, coffee and the Columbus Washboard Company
