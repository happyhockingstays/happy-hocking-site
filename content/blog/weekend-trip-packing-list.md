A weekend trip packing list for a cabin is shorter than you'd think, because the cabin does a lot of the work. The bed is made, the kitchen is stocked with the basics and the hot tub is warm. What you need is the stuff for the trails, the pool and the evenings. Here's the list we'd send a friend.

We host guests every weekend in the Hocking Hills. These are the things they're glad they brought, and the things they didn't need.

## What the cabin already has

Before you pack, cross these off. Every one of our cabins has:

- **Bedroom:** bed linens, extra pillows and blankets, hangers
- **Bathroom:** towels, a hair dryer, shampoo, conditioner and body soap
- **Laundry:** a washer and a dryer
- **Kitchen:** a refrigerator, stove, oven, microwave, dishwasher, pots and pans, dishes and silverware, a toaster, wine glasses and baking sheets
- **Coffee:** a coffee maker and coffee to get you started
- **Outside:** a private hot tub, a fire pit, a grill with utensils, outdoor furniture and a pool that's open in season
- **Everything else:** Wi-Fi, a TV, heat and air conditioning, a first aid kit and free parking

Most cabins also have board games, an iron and a pack 'n play. Check your cabin's page if you need one of those.

## Weekend trip packing list: the essentials

- **Layers.** Hills and gorges run cooler than town. A fleece and a light jacket work in every season but midsummer
- **Hiking shoes with grip.** The single most useful thing you can pack
- **A swimsuit.** The hot tub is open all year, even if the pool isn't
- **Sleepwear and slippers.** Cabin floors get cold
- **Toiletries beyond the basics.** Toothbrush, toothpaste, deodorant, contacts and your own face wash
- **Medications.** Plus a copy of any prescriptions
- **Chargers and a power bank.** Cell service comes and goes in the hills
- **ID and payment.** Plus the booking confirmation with your arrival details

## What to pack for a weekend trip to the trails

The Hocking Hills trails are short, but they aren't paved, except the walk to [Ash Cave](/blog/ash-cave). Most have stone steps and wet sandstone. Pack:

- **Shoes with real tread.** Sneakers with worn soles slip on wet rock
- **Ice cleats in winter.** The park recommends them when the trails ice over
- **A small backpack** with water, snacks and a light rain jacket
- **A headlamp or flashlight.** Trails close a half hour after sunset, and winter days are short
- **Bug spray and sunscreen** in the warm months
- **Downloaded maps.** Save the park map and your cabin's directions before you lose signal

[Old Man's Cave](/blog/old-mans-cave) is the trail most people start with. It's 1 mile with stone steps, so it's worth having the right shoes.

## Food and kitchen

The kitchen has the tools. You bring the food.

- **Groceries for every meal you'll eat in.** Do the big shop on the way. Logan has the supermarkets
- **Your spices and favorite oil.** Basic cooking supplies are there, but bring anything you can't cook without
- **S'mores.** The fire pit is the reason. Some cabins supply firewood, so check your cabin's page
- **A cooler** for the drive and for a picnic at a trailhead
- **Drinks.** Glass stays away from the pool and the hot tub, so pack cans or plastic for outside

## The weekend getaway packing list by season

- **Winter:** a warm coat, hat, gloves, ice cleats and a swimsuit for the hot tub
- **Spring:** a rain jacket, waterproof boots and layers for chilly mornings
- **Summer:** swimsuits, sunscreen, bug spray and pool towels. Pools are open May 15 to October 15, and Clouds Reach's heated pool runs April 15 to November 30
- **Fall:** layers for warm afternoons and cold nights, and a camera for the color

## What to pack on a weekend trip with kids or a group

**With kids:** a favorite blanket, a night-light, swim gear, rain boots and a few small toys for the car. Most of our cabins have a pack 'n play, and some have a high chair. Check the cabin's page first.

**With a group:** a shared list so nobody brings 4 coolers and no coffee filters. Our guide on how to plan a group trip has a trip sheet that covers it.

**For game night:** cards and a favorite board game. [Pickle Hill](/cabins/pickle-hill), [The Farm at Nickel Plate](/cabins/the-farm-at-nickel-plate) and [Lazy Deer Retreat](/cabins/lazy-deer-retreat) have courts, so pack paddles or a ball if you have a favorite.

## For the drive and the arrival

- **Snacks and water** for the car, so nobody arrives hungry
- **Your arrival details.** Check-in is 4:00 pm, and every one of our cabins has self check-in with a keypad, so save the code where you can find it offline
- **A bag for the first night.** Pack pajamas, a toothbrush and a swimsuit on top, so you can get in the hot tub before you unpack
- **A trash bag for the car.** The drive home is easier without a pile of wrappers

## What to leave at home

Our house rules keep the cabins in shape for the next group, so leave these behind:

- **Pets.** None of our cabins allow pets
- **Fireworks, guns and hunting gear.** Not allowed
- **ATVs.** Not allowed
- **Party supplies.** Our cabins don't allow parties or events
- **Extra linens and towels.** The cabin has them

Fires stay in the fire pit, and every cabin is non-smoking. Each cabin lists its own quiet hours.
