# Photos

Updated Nov 9, 2026.

## What was done

Natalie's Dropbox folder (748 photos, 1.5 GB) was turned into web-ready copies. The originals were only read, never changed.

- Resized to 2400px on the long side (smaller photos were left at their own size, never enlarged)
- Saved as JPEG at 80 quality. PNG and HEIC files were converted
- Camera and GPS data removed, so a photo can't reveal an address
- Renamed to the SEO pattern: `the-johnny-bedroom-2-003.jpg`
- Natalie's cabin and category folders kept

Result: 748 photos, 331 MB, in `public/images/cabins/<cabin>/<category>/`. A copy is on Cole's Mac in `Website Photography/_web-ready/`, with contact sheets in `_web-ready/_contact-sheets/`.
